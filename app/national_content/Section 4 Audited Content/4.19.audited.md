# Understanding Novation in Contracts

<!-- When you're dealing with **contracts**, it's important to understand the concept of **novation**. -->
The concept of **novation** applies to contract modifications and party substitutions.

## What is Novation?

**Novation** creates a completely new contract that replaces the original contract. The original contract is terminated and no longer exists.

Novation can happen in two ways:
- Creating an entirely new contract with different terms
- Substituting a new party for one of the original parties in the contract

## Key Points About Novation

- **All parties involved in the original agreement must consent** to the novation.
- The original contract is completely canceled.
- A new contract takes the place of the old contract.
- Novation allows contracts to adapt to new circumstances or new parties.
- It maintains the legal requirements of contractual relationships.

Novation affects the management of changes in contractual relationships while preserving legal integrity.
