# 4.61 Time Is of the Essence

**Time is of the essence** is a clause in a contract that means performance on the exact dates specified is an essential element of the contract. Failure to perform by the date specified is a material breach of the contract.

*   **Strict Deadline:** If a contract states that "time is of the essence" regarding the closing date, the parties must close on that date.
*   **Breach:** If a party fails to close by the specified date, they may be in breach of contract, and the other party may have the right to terminate the contract and keep the earnest money deposit.
*   **Extension:** If the parties need more time, they must agree to an extension in writing.

**Example:**
A purchase agreement states that closing shall take place on or before September 30th, and that "time is of the essence." If the buyer is not ready to close on September 30th, the seller may declare the buyer in default and cancel the contract.
