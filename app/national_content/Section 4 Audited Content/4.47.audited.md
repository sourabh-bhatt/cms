# Mistakes in Contracts

<!-- When you're dealing with **contracts**, it's important to understand how **mistakes** can impact the agreement. -->

**Mistakes** can impact contractual agreements. Mistakes can occur due to errors or misunderstandings about the terms or conditions that are laid out in the contract itself.

## Criteria for a Contract to be Voidable Due to Mistake

For a contract to be considered **voidable** due to a mistake, the error must meet the following conditions:

- The mistake must be **mutual** (both parties are mistaken).
- The mistake must be **unintentional**.
- The mistake must concern a **material fact**.

Example: If both parties unknowingly agree on a contract concerning the incorrect lot of land, this is a situation where a contract could be voidable.

## Mistakes That Do Not Void a Contract

<!-- It is crucial to distinguish that not all mistakes will give you the leeway to void a contract. Specifically: -->

Not all mistakes provide grounds to void a contract. Specifically:

- Mistakes due to **ignorance of the law** are not valid grounds.
- Mistakes stemming from **poor judgment** are also not valid grounds.
