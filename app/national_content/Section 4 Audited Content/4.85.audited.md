# 4.85 Contract Formation in Real Estate

**Contracts come into being** through several main methods of formation in legal agreements.

## Oral Contracts

-   **Oral contracts** are agreements made through spoken promises rather than written ones.
-   A contract does *not* always have to be written to be valid; the basic requirements for creating a contract can be satisfied through verbal agreements.
-   However, oral contracts can be harder to prove if disagreements arise that require court resolution.
-   This difficulty in proving the terms makes oral contracts less reliable in certain scenarios.
-   For the sale of real estate or leases for more than one year, Virginia law requires these agreements to be in writing due to the **statute of frauds**.
-   The statute of frauds exists to prevent fraud and misunderstandings in significant transactions.

## Express and Implied Contracts

-   **Express contracts** have terms and conditions explicitly stated, either orally or in writing. These clear terms leave little room for misinterpretation.
-   **Implied contracts** have terms and conditions that are not directly stated but are inferred from the nature of the contract or the service involved.
-   Implied contracts can sometimes lead to disputes because what was implicitly agreed upon may not be as clear-cut as explicit terms.

## Contract Validity Requirements

These basic concepts of contract formation enable recognition of agreement nuances and demonstrate the need for clarity and proper documentation in legal agreements. This knowledge ensures contracts remain both valid and enforceable.
