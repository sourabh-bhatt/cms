# 4.78 Understanding "Time Is of the Essence" in Sales Contracts

A **sales contract** that sets a **closing time and date** and includes the phrase **"time is of the essence"** creates strict timing requirements for both parties.

## Legal Requirements

-   Both parties must be ready to fulfill their contractual obligations at the specified time
-   If either party fails to perform at the specified moment, it constitutes a **breach of contract**
-   Such a breach might cause the contract to fall apart

## Contract Obligations

The phrase **"time is of the essence"** makes punctuality a *strict contractual obligation* rather than merely advisable timing.
