# Avoiding Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL) in Real Estate Transactions

## Understanding Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL)
<!-- Avoiding **Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL)** is crucial as you navigate through real estate transactions. -->

**Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL)** occurs when non-lawyers perform legal activities. Virginia has laws that prevent the unauthorized practice of law. This typically includes **drafting contracts on behalf of someone else**, specifically those related to real estate, such as **purchase agreements**. Engaging in these activities risks losing a real estate license and facing potential **civil liability** and **criminal penalties**.

## Common Situations and Risks
<!-- As you represent clients, they may request tasks that verge on legal work. For example: -->

Clients may request tasks that constitute legal work. For example:

- Making significant changes to standard sales contracts
- Drafting or altering lease agreements

<!-- In Virginia, these actions could be considered UPL. It is important to: -->

In Virginia, these actions could be considered UPL. Best practices include:

- Stick with **pre-printed contract and lease forms approved by the Virginia Real Estate Board or prepared by attorneys**
- Avoid venturing beyond these forms without proper legal expertise

Doing otherwise could harm your client and expose you to civil liability due to inaccurate drafting.

## When to Refer to a Legal Professional
Certain issues require legal expertise, including:

- Drafting contracts
- Interpreting legal questions
- Addressing title defects
- Handling disclosure problems

<!-- These issues can significantly impact your client's rights and obligations. In such cases, it is your responsibility to advise your client to seek advice from a **legal professional**. -->

These issues can affect client rights and obligations. Real estate agents should advise clients to seek advice from a **legal professional**.

While obtaining legal counsel might:

- Cost your client money
- Potentially disrupt or halt a transaction

<!-- it is vital to prioritize their protection and the legal integrity of the deal. -->

client protection and legal integrity should be prioritized.

## Your Role as a Real Estate Agent

Real estate agents must navigate complex transactions within their professional capabilities. Referring clients to legal professionals when necessary:

- Safeguards client interests
- Protects agents from unauthorized practice of law consequences

Maintaining clear boundaries ensures compliance with legal and ethical standards.
