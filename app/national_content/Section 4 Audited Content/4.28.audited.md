# Understanding the Statute of Frauds in Real Estate Contracts

<!-- When learning about **contracts**, you must understand the **Statute of Frauds**. -->
The **Statute of Frauds** comes from **state laws** based on **common law**. It shows which contracts must be in writing to be **enforceable**.

The **Statute of Frauds** protects parties by requiring a **written agreement** for specific types of transactions. This prevents problems with verbal agreements that are hard to prove.

<!-- When working with contracts, you must know which agreements need to be in writing under the Statute of Frauds. -->
Knowing which agreements need to be in writing under the Statute of Frauds helps ensure contracts are **legally binding** and enforceable in **court**.
