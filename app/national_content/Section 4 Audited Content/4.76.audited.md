# 4.76 Understanding Illegality in Contracts

The concept of **illegality** affects contract validity and enforceability.

## What is an Illegal Contract?

-   A contract that involves actions that break the law is considered **void**.
-   This means that if either party tries to enforce such a contract in court, the court will not support them.

## Legal Implications

-   The law does not stand behind contracts that require someone to act illegally.
-   This principle ensures that agreements remain both ethical and **legally enforceable**.

Example: If a contract requires a party to perform an illegal activity, such as fraud or theft, that contract is void and cannot be enforced by the courts.
