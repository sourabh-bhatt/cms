# 4.79 Fundamental Elements of a Valid Contract

**Contracts** in real estate transactions require basic elements for validity. These elements create clear **mutual understanding** between all parties, with each party knowing exactly what they are agreeing to.

## Key Elements of a Contract

### 1. Offer and Acceptance (Mutual Assent)

-   One party proposes certain terms (**offer**).
-   The other party agrees to those terms (**acceptance**).

This represents a proposal that requires agreement for validity. A contract requires a clear offer from one side and acceptance from the other.

### 2. Consideration

-   Something of value exchanged between the parties.
-   Can be money, services, or a promise to do or not do something.

The element requires **give-and-take** - a trade that benefits both sides. Without this exchange, no valid contract exists.

### 3. Legal Capacity and Legality

-   All parties must have the legal ability to enter into a contract.
-   The contract must be for a legal purpose.

Parties must be competent (of legal age and sound mind). The contract cannot be for illegal activities. These requirements ensure the contract is legally enforceable.

These basic contract elements govern real estate agreements. All agreements must meet these requirements for validity and enforceability.
