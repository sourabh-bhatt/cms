# 4.70 Impact of Death or Insanity on Contract Offers and Formed Contracts

## Meeting of the Minds and Contract Acceptance

Circumstances like **death** or **insanity** impact the validity of an **offer** in **contract negotiations**. Before contract acceptance and formation, both parties must agree completely on terms - a concept known as **"meeting of the minds."** Both parties must clearly comprehend their obligations and promises within the contract.

## Effect of Death or Insanity Before Acceptance

-   If one of the parties to an offer dies or becomes insane **before acceptance**, the mutual understanding required for contract formation cannot be achieved.
-   As a result, the **offer is automatically terminated**.
-   The rationale is that without the **capacity** to understand or consent, no genuine agreement can be formed.
-   This rule exists to ensure fairness and clarity in contract negotiations.

## Effect of Death or Insanity After Contract Formation

-   Once a contract has been formed, if one party dies or becomes insane, the contract does **not** dissolve.
-   Instead, the contract **survives beyond the deceased party's life**.
-   The **obligations and rights** under the contract are passed on to the **estate** of the deceased.
-   The **executor** or responsible party of the deceased's estate must fulfill the obligations the deceased had under the contract.

These principles safeguard transaction integrity and ensure obligation fulfillment in unexpected circumstances.
