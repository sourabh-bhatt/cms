# 4.87 Fraud in the Factum

**Fraud in the Factum** involves *intentional deception* - either through a misleading statement or by hiding the truth - that causes a person to sign a document they did not intend to sign. This type of fraud fundamentally undermines the agreement because there was no true *meeting of the minds*; the party was deceived into signing a document they did not agree to.

As a result:

-   The contract is considered **void**, as if it never existed.
-   This protects parties from being unfairly bound by agreements they were misled into.

This concept shapes the integrity of the **contractual process** and safeguards individuals from fraudulent agreements.
