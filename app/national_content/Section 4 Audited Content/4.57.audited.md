# Non-Legal Insanity and Contract Capacity

<!-- As you dive into the realm of contracts, it's essential to grasp the concept of **_Non-Legal Insanity_**. -->

**Non-Legal Insanity** refers to individuals who are indeed insane but have **not been officially declared so by a court**. 

## Capacity to Contract

- These individuals hold a **limited capacity to engage in contracts**.
- Any contract they enter into is **voidable**, but **not outright void**.

## Voidable vs. Void Contracts

<!-- Understanding this distinction is vital: -->

This distinction affects contract enforcement:

- A **voidable contract** can be **affirmed or rejected** at the option of one party — in this case, the individual whose insanity hasn't been legally confirmed.
- A **void contract** is invalid from the outset and cannot be enforced.
