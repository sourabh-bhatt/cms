# 4.65 Option Contracts

An **Option Contract** is a unilateral contract where a seller (optionor) gives a buyer (optionee) the **exclusive right** to buy the property at a **predetermined price** within a **specific period**.

*   **Unilateral:** Only the seller is obligated to perform. The buyer has the *option* to buy but is not obligated to do so.
*   **Option Fee:** The buyer typically pays a non-refundable option fee for this right. This consideration makes the option binding on the seller.
*   **Exercising the Option:** If the buyer decides to buy (exercises the option), the contract becomes a bilateral purchase agreement.
*   **Expiration:** If the buyer does not exercise the option by the deadline, the option expires, and the seller keeps the option fee.

**Use Case:**
A developer wants to assemble several parcels of land for a project but needs time to check zoning. They might buy options on the parcels to secure the price without being forced to buy if the zoning fails.
