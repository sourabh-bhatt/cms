# 4.77 Election of Remedies in Real Estate Contracts

The **Election of Remedies** concept applies to scenarios involving **vendors** and **vendees**.

## Forfeiture by the Vendor

When a vendor decides to **forfeit** and end the contractual relationship, several consequences occur:

-   The vendor automatically **gives up the right to use their vendor's lien to collect future payments**.
-   The vendor loses the ability to bring an action for **specific performance** of the contract against the vendee. This means the vendor cannot force the buyer to complete the contract through court action.
-   Choosing to forfeit also stops the vendor from using other legal remedies that need the contract to exist.

## Legal Implications

Electing to forfeit a contract means choosing to end the contract instead of using other legal options that require the contract to remain in place. This decision requires consideration of all consequences.
