# 4.68 Understanding the Statute of Frauds in Lease Surrender Agreements

Agreements concerning the **surrender of property** must comply with the **Statute of Frauds**.

## Lease Terms and Agreement Validity

-   For leases with an **unexpired term of one year or less**, **verbal agreements** on surrender are typically considered **valid and enforceable**.
-   For leases that **extend beyond one year**, the agreement must be **documented in writing** to be legally enforceable and comply with the Statute of Frauds.

These requirements govern lease surrender agreements and legal compliance.
