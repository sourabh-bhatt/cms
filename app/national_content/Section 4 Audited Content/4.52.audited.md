# Unilateral Contracts in Real Estate

In a **unilateral contract** scenario, only one party makes a promise to perform specific actions under certain conditions. 

## Unilateral Contracts

- The deal begins as an **offer** from one side.
- There is no agreement between two parties until the other side chooses to **accept** the offer through their actions.

## Example of a Unilateral Contract

Example: A classic example involves someone promising a reward in exchange for a particular behavior or action, such as:

> "I will give $50 to the person who returns my lost dog."
