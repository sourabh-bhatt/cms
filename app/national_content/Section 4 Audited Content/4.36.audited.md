# Understanding Executory and Executed Contracts in Real Estate

<!-- When you enter into contracts during your transactions, it's crucial to understand the difference between **executory** and **executed contracts**. Let's break down these concepts so you can navigate them confidently. -->

Contracts fall into two categories: **executory** and **executed contracts**. These distinctions affect how agreements function in real estate transactions.

## Executory Contract

An **executory contract** exists when all parties agree on terms but have not yet carried out those terms. A common example is a **sales contract**, where the agreement is in place, but the actual sale and transfer of ownership have yet to be completed.

## Executed Contract

An **executed contract** is one where all parties have signed the agreement. Once signed, the contract becomes binding on all parties.
