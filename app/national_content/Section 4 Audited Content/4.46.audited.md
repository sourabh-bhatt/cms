# Offers and Option Contracts in Real Estate

<!-- When you're presenting an **offer**, remember that as the person making the offer, you can **retract it at any moment before it gets accepted** by the recipient. This is crucial to understand because it emphasizes the *temporary nature of offers*. -->

The person making an **offer** can **retract it at any moment before it gets accepted** by the recipient. This emphasizes the *temporary nature of offers*. 

## Exception: Option Contracts

<!-- However, keep in mind there's an exception to this rule. If your offer is part of a valid **option contract**, then withdrawing it isn't as straightforward. -->

However, there's an exception to this rule. If the offer is part of a valid **option contract**, then withdrawing it isn't as straightforward. An option contract is:

- A promise **not to revoke the offer** for a certain period
- Made **in exchange for consideration**
- Binding the offeror to the offer for that duration

## Impact of Retracting an Offer

If the offeror decides to pull back the offer before it's accepted:

- The recipient loses their chance to accept it
- This effectively eliminates the possibility of a contract based on the withdrawn offer
