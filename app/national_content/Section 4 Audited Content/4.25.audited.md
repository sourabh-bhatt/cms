# Effect of Death or Incapacity on Offers and Contracts

<!-- When dealing with **offers** in the context of contracts, it's important to understand how certain events can affect them. -->
Certain events can affect **offers** in the context of contracts.

## Termination of Offers Due to Death or Incapacity

- If either party involved in a potential contract **dies** or becomes **incapacitated** before the offer is accepted, the offer will **immediately terminate**.
- This is because a contract requires a **"meeting of the minds"**, which means a mutual understanding and agreement on what each party is committing to do or refrain from doing.
- If one party can no longer physically or mentally engage in the contract due to death or insanity, this mutual understanding cannot occur, leading to termination of the offer.

## Contracts After Acceptance and Death of a Party

- If the offer was accepted and a **contract** was formed, the situation changes.
- A contract does **not** automatically terminate upon the death of a party.
- The contract remains **valid**, and the obligations of the deceased party fall to their **estate**.
- The **executor** or another representative of the estate is responsible for ensuring the deceased's contractual obligations are fulfilled.
