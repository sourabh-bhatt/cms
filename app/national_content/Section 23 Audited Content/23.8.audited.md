# Private Membership Clubs and Fair Housing in Virginia

<!-- When you work with **housing legislation** in Virginia, you need to understand how **private membership clubs** operate. -->

Working with **housing legislation** in Virginia requires understanding how **private membership clubs** operate. These clubs can restrict their facilities to members only under certain conditions.

## Legal Basis for Private Club Exemptions

The **Virginia Fair Housing Act** allows private membership clubs to restrict their facilities to members only. This includes lodging facilities. The club must meet specific requirements:

- The club must be a **bona fide private membership club**
- The club must not be open to the general public
- The club's facilities are for members and their guests only

## Requirements for Private Club Status

For a club to qualify for this exemption:

- Members must pay dues or fees
- The club must have membership requirements
- The club must operate primarily for social, recreational, or similar purposes
- The club cannot be a cover for discrimination in commercial operations

## Implications for Real Estate Professionals

<!-- When you work with properties affiliated with private clubs, remember: -->

Working with properties affiliated with private clubs involves:

- These clubs can legally restrict facilities to members only
- The club must maintain its **bona fide private membership** status
- This exemption applies to all club facilities, including lodging
- The exemption does not apply if the club operates commercially
