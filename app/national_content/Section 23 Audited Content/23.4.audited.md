# Redlining and Fair Lending Practices

**Redlining** refers to the illegal practice where a lender refuses a loan based on the location of the property, particularly if the property is in a neighborhood perceived as deteriorating. 

## Key Points on Loan Approval

- It is **illegal** for lenders to deny loans solely because of the property's location.
- The only factors that should influence loan approval are:
  - The **economic qualifications** of the applicant.
  - The **appraised value** of the property.

This ensures that loan decisions are **fair** and based on **objective criteria**, rather than subjective or discriminatory views about certain neighborhoods.
