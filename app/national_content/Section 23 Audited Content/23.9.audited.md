# Rights and Responsibilities Regarding Assistance Animals in Housing

<!-- When discussing the rights and responsibilities concerning the use of an **assistance animal** in a living space, it's crucial to understand the key points outlined in **Â§ 36-96.3:1**. This section provides important guidance for housing situations involving assistance animals. -->

The rights and responsibilities concerning the use of an **assistance animal** in a living space are outlined in **Â§ 36-96.3:1**. This section provides guidance for housing situations involving assistance animals.

## Use of Assistance Animals in Rental Properties

- If a person with a disability uses an assistance animal in their home, they must follow the **rental agreement** and any rules set forth by the property owner.
- **No pet fees, deposits, or additional rent** can be charged for the presence of an assistance animal.
- However, tenants are **responsible for any damages** the assistance animal causes to the property.

## When Can an Assistance Animal Be Denied?

An assistance animal may be denied if:

1. The animal poses a **clear and present threat of substantial harm** to others or to the property.
2. This risk is **beyond breed, size, or type** considerations.
3. The risk **cannot be mitigated by other reasonable means**.

These determinations are made on a **case-by-case basis**.

## Verification of Disability and Need for Assistance Animal

- If the disability is **obvious or already known** to the housing provider, they **cannot require additional proof** of the disability or the need for the assistance animal.
- This policy **streamlines the accommodation process** and **protects the privacy** of individuals with disabilities.
