# Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)

The **Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)**, signed into law in 1990, is a pivotal piece of legislation that ensures **civil rights protections** for individuals with disabilities. These protections are comparable to those based on race, sex, national origin, and religion.

## Purpose of the ADA

The ADA is designed to guarantee **equal opportunities** for people with disabilities across various areas of life, including:

- **Employment**
- **Accessing public spaces**
- **Transportation**
- **State and local government services**
- **Telecommunications**
