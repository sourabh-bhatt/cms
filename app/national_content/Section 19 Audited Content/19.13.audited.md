# In-Person Closing and Title Transfer

<!-- An **in-person closing** involves completing the essential steps required to transfer the **title** of a property at a predetermined time and place. -->

An **in-person closing** involves completing the steps required to transfer the **title** of a property at a predetermined time and place.

## Key Steps in the Closing Process

- The **grantor** (seller) will deliver the **deed** to the **grantee** (buyer) upon **settlement**.
- This step ensures the **legal transfer of property ownership**.

<!-- Understanding how to manage and smoothly navigate this process is key to successfully finalizing property deals and is a fundamental aspect of **real estate transactions** you'll be involved in. -->

Managing this process supports successful property transaction completion in **real estate transactions**.
