# Physical Description of Property and Its Role in Real Estate Taxes

Real estate taxes involve the term **"Physical Description of Property."** This concept provides the groundwork for understanding how local tax assessment offices calculate annual property taxes.

## What Does the Physical Description Encompass?

The physical description includes detailed specifications of both the **land** and **building area**:

- **Building's square footage**
- **Land's square footage or acreage**
- Number of **bedrooms** and **bathrooms**
- Features such as **fireplaces**, **decks**, **patios**, **garages**, and **basements**

## Construction Materials and Types

Beyond the basic layout, the physical description also covers the materials and construction types, including:

- **Roofing materials** (e.g., composition shingle, slate, or other materials)
- **Exterior wall materials** (e.g., siding, shingles, brick, stone, or others)
- **Construction type and style**, such as **single-family** or **2-story homes**

## Property Tax Assessment Applications

A property's physical description forms the basis upon which local authorities assess property taxes. Each detail - from square footage to construction materials - plays a role in determining the annual taxes levied on a property.

Interpreting these descriptions enables informed decisions and accurate advice regarding property taxes in real estate practice.
