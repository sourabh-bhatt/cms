# Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA) and Its Exemptions

<!-- You need to understand the **Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA)**. RESPA affects how certain transactions are conducted and what disclosures must be made. However, not every transaction falls under RESPA's umbrella. Learning these **exemptions** will help you in real estate practice. -->
The **Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA)** affects how certain transactions are conducted and what disclosures must be made. Not every transaction falls under RESPA's umbrella. These **exemptions** apply to real estate practice.

## Transactions Exempt from RESPA

<!-- Let's clarify which transactions RESPA doesn't apply to: -->
Transactions RESPA doesn't apply to:

- **Business purpose loans**: Loans primarily for commercial, rather than personal, family, or household purposes.
- **Temporary financing**: Such as bridge loans.
- **Purchase of vacant land**.
- **Loan conversions**.
- **Secondary market transfers**.
- **Transactions financed solely by the seller**.
- **Loan assumptions without lender approval**.
- **Residential properties with more than four dwelling units**.
- **Cash sales**.

## RESPA Exemptions in Practice

RESPA requires borrowers to receive detailed **disclosures** when getting a mortgage. This includes information on **loan settlement costs**, and it delineates specific practices that are not allowed.

Knowing which transactions RESPA does not apply to helps navigate these dealings more smoothly and ensures **compliance with the law**. This knowledge enables accurate client guidance, providing a clear picture of obligations and rights in transactions that either fall within or outside RESPA's scope.
