# Understanding Tax Rates in Real Estate

<!-- When you're diving into real estate, understanding how **tax rates** work is crucial. **Tax rates** determine the amount of tax you owe to a **taxing authority**. -->
**Tax rates** determine the amount of tax owed to a **taxing authority**.

## How Tax Rates Are Determined

Each taxing body—such as **school districts** or **water districts**—operates as a separate entity that calculates its own tax rate. Here's the process:

1. The amount of money needed by each **taxing district** is calculated.
2. This amount is divided by the total **assessed value** of all the real estate in the area.
3. The result is a percentage representing the district's **tax rate**.

## Receiving Your Property Tax Bill

<!-- - Typically, as a property owner, you will receive a **single tax bill** covering all taxing authorities.
- However, in some regions, you might receive **separate bills** from each taxing authority involved. -->
- Property owners typically receive a **single tax bill** covering all taxing authorities.
- However, in some regions, property owners might receive **separate bills** from each taxing authority involved.
- **Deadlines** for paying these taxes are established by law and are not arbitrary.

## How Tax Rates Are Expressed

Tax rates are usually expressed in one of two ways:

- As a **percentage** (e.g., 5%)
- As a specific amount per $100 of assessed value (e.g., $5 per $100)
