# Understanding Property Taxes in Real Estate

<!-- Let's dive into understanding the ins and outs of **property taxes**, a crucial aspect you need to grasp in real estate. When dealing with properties, figuring out taxes, their rates, and assessed values are daily tasks. Here's how you'll do it. -->
**Property taxes** represent a key aspect in real estate. When dealing with properties, calculating taxes, their rates, and assessed values are daily tasks.

## Calculating the Tax Rate

Example: Consider a property valued at $100,000, with $5,600 in annual property taxes. To find the **tax rate**, use the formula:

> **Property Taxes ÷ Assessed Value = Tax Rate**

In practice, it looks like this:

- Divide $5,600 by $100,000, which gives you 0.056.
- To express this as a percentage, multiply by 100 or shift the decimal two places to the right, resulting in a **5.6% tax rate**.

<!-- Understanding this percentage is foundational, as it reflects the portion of the property's value owed in taxes each year. -->
This percentage reflects the portion of the property's value owed in taxes each year.

## Calculating Annual Property Taxes

<!-- When you know a property's assessed value and the tax rate, you can calculate the annual property taxes. For example: -->
Knowing a property's assessed value and the tax rate enables calculation of annual property taxes. Example:

- Assessed Value: $100,000
- Tax Rate: $5.60 for every $100 of value

Steps:

1. Convert the rate per hundred to a decimal:
   
   $5.60 ÷ $100 = 0.056

2. Multiply this rate by the assessed value:

   0.056 × $100,000 = $5,600

The result is the **annual tax obligation** based on the property's valuation and the applicable rate.

## Calculating Assessed Value

<!-- If you have the annual tax and tax rate, you can find the assessed value of a property. For example: -->
Having the annual tax and tax rate enables determination of a property's assessed value. Example:

- Annual Taxes: $5,600
- Tax Rate: $5.60 per $100

Use the formula:

> **Property Tax ÷ Tax Rate = Assessed Value**

Steps:

1. Convert the tax rate to a decimal:

   $5.60 ÷ $100 = 0.056

2. Divide the property tax by the decimal rate:

   $5,600 ÷ 0.056 = $100,000

This calculation helps in evaluating a property's worth against its tax obligations.
