# Understanding Federal Tax Deductions in Real Estate

<!-- When working in real estate, understanding **federal tax deductions** is key. As a homeowner, you can deduct the following from your federal income tax: -->
**Federal tax deductions** apply in real estate. Homeowners can deduct the following from federal income tax:

- **Mortgage interest payments**
- **Real estate taxes**
- Some closing costs, such as **points** paid to obtain a mortgage
