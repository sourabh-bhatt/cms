# Usury Laws in Real Estate

<!-- When it comes to understanding real estate, it's important to learn about **usury laws**. These are state laws designed to prevent excessively high interest rates from being charged. Essentially, **usury laws** set a maximum limit on the **interest rates** that can be applied to **loans**. -->
**Usury laws** are state laws designed to prevent excessively high interest rates from being charged. These laws set a maximum limit on the **interest rates** that can be applied to **loans**.

## Virginia Usury Laws

In Virginia, usury laws are found in **Virginia Code § 6.2-303**. Virginia sets specific limits on interest rates:

- **General maximum rate**: 12% per year
- **Small loans under $2,500**: Up to 36% per year
- **Certain licensed lenders**: May charge higher rates under specific regulations

## Application in Real Estate

<!-- As a real estate agent, you should understand that: -->
Real estate agents should understand that:

- **Seller financing** arrangements must comply with Virginia usury limits
- **Private lenders** cannot exceed legal interest rate caps
- Violations can make loan contracts **void and unenforceable**
