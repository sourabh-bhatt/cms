# Understanding New Home Warranties

<!-- When you're guiding your clients through the process of purchasing a new home, it's essential to educate them about the **warranties** that come with it. **Warranties** for new homes can be provided by the **builder** or purchased from an **independent company**. These warranties offer limited coverage for various aspects of the home, should anything not meet standard expectations.

## Warranty Coverage Breakdown

- **First Year:** Coverage on **workmanship** and **materials**. This includes parts of the home like **siding** and **drywall**.
- **First Two Years:** Warranty extends to the home's **systems**, including:
  - Air conditioning
  - Heating
  - Plumbing
  - Electrical systems
- **Up to 10 Years:** Coverage for major **structural defects**. Example: If a roof suddenly fails, this part of the warranty provides protection.

## What Warranties Typically Do Not Cover

- **Cosmetic or Non-Structural Defects:** Such as small cracks in brickwork or tiles that are slightly off color.
- Items already covered by a **manufacturer's warranty**, including appliances, furnace, or hot water heater.
