# Understanding Equity in Home Ownership

<!-- When you're exploring the concept of **home ownership**, it's essential to understand **equity**. -->
**Equity** is a fundamental concept in **home ownership**.

## What is Equity?

Equity represents the ownership stake in a property. It is the difference between the current market value of the home and the amount owed on the mortgage.

## Example

<!-- - Imagine you buy a house for **$100,000**.
- You make a **$20,000 down payment**.
- You secure an **$80,000 mortgage** to finance the rest. -->
Example: A buyer purchases a house for **$100,000**, makes a **$20,000 down payment**, and secures an **$80,000 mortgage** to finance the rest.

Initially, the equity in the house is **$20,000** (the down payment).

## Equity Growth Over Time

Ten years later:

- The house value has increased to **$150,000**.
- The owner has paid **$40,000 towards the principal** of the mortgage.

At the time of sale, equity is calculated as:

**Equity = Current Market Value - Remaining Mortgage Debt**

- Current Market Value = $150,000
- Remaining Mortgage Debt = $40,000 ($80,000 - $40,000)

Therefore:

**Equity = $150,000 - $40,000 = $110,000**

This $110,000 represents the cash value in the home at the time of sale.

## Equity Applications

- Equity helps inform decisions about buying and selling property.
- Demonstrates the impact of **property value appreciation** over time.
- Shows the importance of paying down the **principal** on mortgages to increase equity.
