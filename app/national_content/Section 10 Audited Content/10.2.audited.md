# Understanding Home Warranties

<!-- When you guide your clients through the home buying process, it's crucial to explain the role of **home warranties**. -->
The role of **home warranties** affects the home buying process.

## Role of Home Warranties

- Home warranties do **not** absolve the seller or agent from the obligation to disclose any significant defects in the property.
- They offer a layer of protection by serving as a shield against claims of **misrepresentation**.
- Home warranties provide buyers with a means to address specific issues that may emerge **post-settlement**, helping to mitigate potential disputes.
