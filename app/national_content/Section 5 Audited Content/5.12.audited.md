# 5.12 Bargain and Sale Deed in Property Transactions

Property transactions involve different types of **deeds**. The **Bargain and Sale Deed** represents one such type.

## What is a Bargain and Sale Deed?

A Bargain and Sale Deed contains **no express warranties** from the seller (also known as the **grantor**) regarding the quality or status of the **property title** being transferred.

- With a **Bargain and Sale Deed**, the seller implies they hold title and possession of the property.
- However, the deed contains **no warranties against encumbrances** - the seller makes no promises about liens, mortgages, or other claims against the property.
- The seller provides no protection against problems from any time period - neither during their ownership nor before.

While it implies a level of **ownership interest** that can be transferred, the buyer receives no warranty protection and must ensure that the title is clear of any issues through their own due diligence.

Note that Bargain and Sale Deeds are not commonly used in Virginia residential real estate transactions. Most Virginia transactions use General Warranty Deeds or Special Warranty Deeds.
