# 5.32 Patent Deeds

A **Patent Deed** is a document that transfers property from the **government** to an individual. This deed marks the transfer of ownership from a **public entity** to a **private person**.

Patent Deeds show how land moves from public ownership to private ownership. This transfer gives the private owner legal claim to the property.

Patent Deeds show how land that was once part of the **public domain** becomes privately owned property.
