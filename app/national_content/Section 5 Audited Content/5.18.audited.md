# 5.18 Seisin in Real Estate

The concept of **seisin** applies to real estate.

## What is Seisin?

Seisin refers to the covenant of seisin, which is a promise made by the person selling a property (the **grantor**) to the person buying the property (the **grantee**). This promise guarantees that the seller:

-   Owns the property to the degree they claim to
-   Has the right to sell the property

The covenant of seisin assures the buyer that the seller can legally transfer ownership.

## Breach of the Covenant of Seisin

This covenant can be broken. A breach occurs if:

-   The actual ownership of the property belongs to someone else, not the seller
-   The seller does not own the property to the extent they have claimed

Such breaches can impact the transaction and real estate professional responsibilities.

## Seisin Benefits

Seisin helps ensure that properties have a clear and rightful ownership status, avoiding potential legal complications.
