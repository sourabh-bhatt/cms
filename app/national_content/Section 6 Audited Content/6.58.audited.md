# Original Buyer Liability in Mortgages

**Original buyer liability** refers to the roles and responsibilities involved when a mortgage is transferred.

## Key Concepts

- The person who takes over a mortgage is known as the **assuming buyer**.
- If the assuming buyer fails to make payments, the original borrower remains responsible for the debt.
- The **lender** has the right to take legal action against:
  - The original borrower,
  - The assuming buyer, or
  - Both parties together,
  to recover any outstanding amounts.

This concept in **mortgages** and **deeds of trust** highlights the enduring nature of the **original borrower's obligations** even after transferring the property.
