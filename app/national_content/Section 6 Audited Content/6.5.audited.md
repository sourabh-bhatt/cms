# Understanding the Reduction Certificate in Real Estate Transactions

A **Reduction Certificate**, also called a **payoff statement**, provides current loan status information for properties in real estate transactions. This document contains loan details as of a specific date.

## Key Information Found in a Reduction Certificate

Reduction Certificates contain the following information:

- The **outstanding balance** of the loan
- The loan's **interest rate**
- The loan's **maturity date**
- The **monthly payment amount**
- Any **prepayment penalties**

## Reduction Certificate Applications

This document serves specific purposes when:

- Assuming the payments of an existing loan
- Paying off a loan at closing

The Reduction Certificate allows verification of loan details and provides necessary information for transaction decisions.

The Reduction Certificate helps facilitate real estate transaction processes.
