# Assignment and Novation in Mortgages and Deeds of Trust

**Lenders** can change positions within **mortgages** and **deeds of trust** agreements by transferring their interest in lending agreements to different parties. This process is known as an **assignment**.

## Assignment

- **Definition:** Assignment is when the rights of a contractual agreement are moved from one party to another.
- **Purpose:** Allows lenders to transfer their interest in the lending agreement.

## Novation

**Lenders** can also alter agreement positions through **novation**.

- **Definition:** Novation is the act of substituting one agreement for another.
- **Key Outcome:** The original agreement is completely canceled and replaced with a new one.
- **Benefit:** Allows for a fresh start with a new set of terms, which could be beneficial in certain circumstances.

## Assignment and Novation Applications

**Assignment** and **novation** affect real estate processes through:

- Facilitating discussions and transactions involving lenders.
- Managing party interests during agreement transitions and evolving circumstances.
