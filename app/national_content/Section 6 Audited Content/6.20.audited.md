# Understanding the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA)

The **Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA)** governs specific loan types in real estate transactions.

## What is RESPA?

RESPA covers loans secured with mortgages on **residential properties intended for one to four families**. This includes various loan types:

- Most **purchase loans**
- **Assumptions**
- **Refinances**
- **Property improvement loans**
- **Equity lines of credit**

## RESPA Applications

RESPA knowledge supports client guidance through transactions while ensuring **regulatory** compliance. This applies when working with different loan types and determining RESPA coverage.

This knowledge supports various real estate market scenarios and promotes compliant transactions.
