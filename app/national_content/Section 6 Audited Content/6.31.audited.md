# Loan Assumptions in Real Estate Financing

Borrowers can finance property through **loan assumptions**, where buyers take over existing loans - either **mortgages** or **deeds of trust (DOT)**.

## How Loan Assumptions Work

Buyers may choose to assume existing loans rather than obtain new financing for several reasons:

- Potentially securing a lower interest rate
- Avoiding the cost of new loan origination fees

Loan assumptions allow buyers to take over existing loan **obligations and terms**, continuing payments under the same conditions as original borrowers.

**Important Note:** Most conventional loans today contain due-on-sale clauses that prevent assumptions without lender consent. FHA and VA loans are more commonly assumable.

## Loan Assumption Requirements

**Lender approval** is necessary for loan assumptions. Buyers must sign formal assumption agreements and meet lender criteria, which may include:

- Creditworthiness checks
- Income verification
- Reassessment of the property's value

Professional requirements include:

- Facilitating communication between all parties
- Understanding the legal implications
- Ensuring the paperwork accurately and legally reflects the assumption

## Loan Assumption Applications

Loan assumptions provide financing strategies that offer benefits under appropriate circumstances and flexible financing options for property purchases.
