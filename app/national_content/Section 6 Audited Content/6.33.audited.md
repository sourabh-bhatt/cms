# Understanding Home Equity Loans

**Home equity loans** function within **mortgages** and **deeds of trust** frameworks. These loans can serve as first, second, or third mortgages on properties and offer either **fixed** or **adjustable interest rates**.

### What Makes Home Equity Loans Unique?

- They are **secured by the equity in real estate**, meaning the real estate itself serves as **collateral** for the loan.

### Payment Options for Home Equity Loans

Lenders providing home equity loans can:

- Make a **single payment** to borrowers, or
- Allow borrowers to **draw payments from a line of credit**.

This flexibility provides borrowers with multiple funding access options.

### Common Uses of Home Equity Loans

These loans commonly serve to **consolidate consumer debt** with the following benefits:

- The interest paid on mortgage debt, like a home equity loan, **may be tax deductible**.
- Interest on most consumer debts is **not tax deductible**.

Borrowers should consult with tax professionals about their specific situation.

These details support informed decision-making about leveraging real estate equity.
