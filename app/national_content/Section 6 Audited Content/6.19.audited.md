# Understanding Second Mortgages in Real Estate Transactions

**Second mortgages** function within **mortgages** and **deeds of trust** frameworks in Virginia real estate transactions.

## What Are Second Mortgages?

Second mortgages are loans that exist alongside **first mortgages**. They are *subordinate* to the first mortgage. This means the **first mortgage** gets priority over any **second mortgages** for repayment. This happens when a borrower defaults and the property must be sold to repay debts.

## Visualizing Loan Priority

Example: Consider stacking cups where each cup represents a loan.

- The first cup placed on the table is the **first mortgage**.
- Any cups stacked on top represent **second mortgages**.

If you need to remove cups, you always start from the top (**second mortgages**) before you can remove the bottom one (**first mortgage**).

## Second Mortgage Applications

This concept affects several areas:

- The **borrower's ability** to secure additional financing.
- The **lender's risk** in providing such loans.
- **Interest rates** - second mortgages typically have higher rates due to increased risk.

Virginia real estate professionals use loan interaction knowledge to advise clients on **second mortgage** decisions and property investments with existing financial structures.
