# Covenant to Pay Insurance

The **"Covenant to Pay Insurance"** represents a promise **borrowers** make to maintain **insurance coverage** on **financed properties**.

## Key Points of the Covenant to Pay Insurance

- The insurance must protect against **damage or destruction** to the property.
- The **coverage amount** is determined by the **lender**.
- The **lender** is named as an **additional insured or mortgagee** on the insurance policy.

This arrangement ensures that in case of any damage or destruction, the lender's investment in the property is secured because they have **rights to the insurance proceeds**, up to the **balance of the loan**. 

This concept demonstrates property and financial interest protection mechanisms.
