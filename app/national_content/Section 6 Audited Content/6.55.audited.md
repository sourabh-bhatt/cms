# Blanket Mortgages and Partial Release Clauses

A **_Blanket Mortgage_** is a mortgage that covers multiple properties as collateral for a single loan.

## What is a Blanket Mortgage?

Example: When dealing with multiple properties that need to be used as collateral for a loan, you can use a blanket mortgage instead of taking out separate mortgages for each property. A blanket mortgage allows borrowers to pledge several parcels of real estate as security against a single debt.

## Key Feature: Partial Release Clause

The **_partial release clause_** enables the release of a single parcel from the mortgage agreement once a certain amount of the loan has been repaid.

- This allows borrowers to reduce their debt incrementally.
- It frees up individual properties from the mortgage as payments are made.

## Practical Use Among Builders and Developers

Blanket mortgages are a popular tool among builders and developers.

- Example: When overseeing the development of a large tract of land that will eventually be divided into individual plots for sale, you can use a blanket mortgage to finance the whole project under a single loan.
- As parts of the land are developed and sold, the partial release clause releases specific parcels from the mortgage based on loan repayment.

Blanket mortgages provide advantages for managing larger-scale real estate deals or development projects.
