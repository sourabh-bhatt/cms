# Mortgage Assumption and the Assumption Clause

The **assumption process** operates within **mortgages** and **deeds of trust** structures.

## What is Mortgage Assumption?

Mortgage assumption occurs when a buyer takes over the seller's existing mortgage payments and loan obligations. The buyer becomes responsible for the remaining loan balance.

## Lender Approval Requirements

- The assumption process typically requires the **lender's approval**.
- This approval depends on the loan terms in the original mortgage agreement.
- Many modern mortgages include a **due-on-sale clause** that prohibits assumption.
- The lender decides whether to allow the mortgage transfer to a new buyer.

## Mortgage Assumption Applications

- Mortgage assumption affects **buying or selling strategies**.
- In some cases, assuming an existing mortgage might offer benefits over obtaining a new loan, such as potentially better interest rates or terms.

Example: If you assume a mortgage with a lower interest rate than current market rates, you could realize savings over the loan's life.

These concepts support informed decision-making in real estate transactions involving existing mortgages.
