# Handling Excess Proceeds in Foreclosure and Trustee Sales

Properties undergoing **foreclosure** or **trustee sales** due to **delinquencies** generate specific procedures for funds exceeding owed amounts.

## Excess Proceeds Explained

**Excess proceeds** from sales - money remaining after settling all **unpaid claims** against **foreclosed properties** - belong to defaulted **borrowers**. This principle allows **lenders** to recoup owed amounts while protecting borrowers' remaining property **equity**.

## Excess Funds Applications

Excess fund procedures affect foreclosure and trustee sale transactions by establishing rights and responsibilities for all parties involved.
