# Understanding Mortgages in Real Estate

A **mortgage** functions as a financing mechanism in real estate transactions. When property purchases require financing beyond available funds, mortgages provide the necessary capital. A mortgage is a **loan** secured by a **voluntary lien** on the property being purchased.

## What is a Mortgage?

A mortgage is an agreement where borrowers voluntarily give lenders the right to claim properties when borrowers cannot fulfill **repayment terms** in loan agreements. Borrowers receive property access in exchange for commitment to repay borrowed amounts under agreed conditions.

## Ownership During Mortgage Repayment

State laws determine ownership arrangements during mortgage repayment through two different approaches:

### Lien Theory

- In most states, under the **lien theory**, you hold onto the **title** of your property during and after you've finished repaying the loan.
- This means, "Yes, I owe you, but this property is still mine while I pay you back."

### Title Theory

- In a few states, under the **title theory**, the lender holds the title to the property until you've paid off the mortgage.
- The lender holds the legal title and transfers it back to you once the mortgage is paid off.

Understanding these mechanisms gives you a solid foundation in navigating the complexities of real estate transactions and helping your clients through their property purchasing journey.
