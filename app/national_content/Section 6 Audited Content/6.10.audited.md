# Wraparound Mortgage

A **wraparound mortgage** combines new borrowed money with the outstanding balance of an existing mortgage.

## How a Wraparound Mortgage Works

- Instead of paying off the older mortgage, a new, larger mortgage is created that **"wraps around"** the existing one.
- This setup allows for a **single payment** to cover both loans.

## Position of the New Mortgage

- The new mortgage holds a **junior position** to the older mortgage.
- Borrowers make payments to the wraparound mortgage lender.
- This lender then makes payments on the original loan.

This arrangement is designed to safeguard the new lender's interests despite their junior status.

## Wraparound Mortgage Structure

Wraparound mortgages provide a solution for combining debts while allowing both lenders and borrowers to achieve their financial objectives.
