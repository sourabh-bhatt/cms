# Joint Tenancy in Property Ownership

<!-- Removed the below para -->

<!-- In your exploration of property ownership, you'll encounter an important concept: **Joint Tenancy**. This is where the title of a property is shared among multiple people, yet remarkably, they all hold it as though they were just one person. -->

**Joint Tenancy** is a form of property ownership where the title is shared among multiple people, but each holds it as if they were a single owner.

## Equal Rights Among Joint Tenants

<!-- When delving into joint tenancy, it's crucial to recognize that every joint tenant enjoys **equal rights** to the property. This means each of you can fully use and occupy the space—this is known as the **right of possession**. -->

In joint tenancy, every joint tenant has equal rights to the property. This includes the right of possession, allowing each tenant full use and occupancy of the property.

## Right of Survivorship

A key characteristic that defines joint tenancy is its automatic transfer of interest upon a tenant's death, called the **right of survivorship**. In simpler terms:

- If one joint tenant passes away, their interest in the property does **not** go through a will or estate.
- Instead, the interest directly transfers to the surviving joint tenants.
