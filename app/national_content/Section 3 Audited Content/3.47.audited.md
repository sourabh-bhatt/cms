# Freehold Estates in Property Rights

<!-- A **freehold estate** is an important concept in **property rights**. A freehold estate means you own an interest in land. You have both possession and ownership rights that last for an indefinite time.

When you hold a freehold estate, you are **"seized"** of the land. This term, **_seisin_**, signifies both the possession and ownership of a freehold estate. -->

A **freehold estate** represents ownership interest in land. Freehold estates provide both possession and ownership rights for an indefinite time.

When someone holds a freehold estate, they are **"seized"** of the land. The term **_seisin_** signifies both possession and ownership of a freehold estate.

## Types of Freehold Estates

<!-- There are two main varieties of freehold estates that you'll encounter:

- **Fee Estates**
- **Life Estates**

Each type defines how much ownership you have and under what conditions. Understanding these concepts helps you work with real estate ownership. -->

Two main varieties of freehold estates exist:

- **Fee Estates**
- **Life Estates**

Each type defines ownership extent and conditions.
