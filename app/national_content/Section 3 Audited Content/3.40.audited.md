# Right of First Refusal in Property Rights

<!-- When learning about **property rights**, it's important to understand the concept of the **Right of First Refusal**. -->

## What is the Right of First Refusal?

Imagine entering into an agreement with a property owner. This agreement gives the first chance to buy or lease the property if the owner decides to sell or lease it to someone else.

When a third party makes an offer, the owner must gives the chance to match that offer before accepting it.

## Key Distinction from an Option Contract

- With a **Right of First Refusal**, the property owner is _not_ obliged to sell or lease the property.
- The owner does _not_ have to decide to lease or sell the property simply because this right exists.
- The owner has no obligation until they receive an offer from someone else.

This setup allows being in a preferential position without obligating the owner to act.
