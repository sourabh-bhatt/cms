# Understanding Pur Autre Vie in Property Rights

<!-- When learning about **property rights**, it's important to understand various terms, one of which is **"Pur Autre Vie."** This is a French phrase that means *"for another's life."* It is used to describe a type of **life estate**. -->

When learning about **property rights**, understanding various terms is necessary, including **"Pur Autre Vie."** This is a French phrase that means *"for another's life."* It is used to describe a type of **life estate**.

## What is a Life Estate?

In a typical **life estate**, the rights to use and benefit from a property last for the lifetime of the person holding the estate.

## Pur Autre Vie Life Estate

However, in a **Pur Autre Vie** life estate, the duration isn't based on the life of the holder of the estate, but rather on the life of another, a third party.
