The **Statute of Limitations** represents a fundamental concept in **property rights**. These **state laws** set the **time frame** within which someone can file a **lawsuit**, including actions to enforce a **contract**. If someone attempts to file a lawsuit to enforce a contract after this designated period has expired, courts will not hear the case.

## Purpose of the Statute of Limitations

These laws help business and commerce by creating a cutoff point for **legal liabilities**. This means that after a certain period, parties cannot face legal action for old agreements. This helps business move forward without worry about old disputes.
