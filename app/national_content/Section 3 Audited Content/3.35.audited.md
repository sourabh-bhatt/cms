# Life Tenant Responsibilities and Restrictions

<!-- As a **life tenant**, you hold a **life estate** for the span of your own life. It's important to understand what you can and cannot do under this arrangement. -->

A **life tenant** holds a **life estate** for the duration of their lifetime. Certain rights and limitations apply under this arrangement.

## Prohibition Against Waste

Just like other tenants, which are prohibited from committing **waste**, which means they cannot make improper use or abuse the property. This is crucial because individuals with a **future interest** in the property have the right to sue to prevent or resolve any instances of waste.

## Responsibilities of a Life Tenant

These responsibilities include:

- **Maintaining the premises** in good condition for any future interest holders. This means keeping the property in a condition that respects the rights of those who will inherit it after the life tenant’s lifetime.
- Paying any **tax or mortgage fees** associated with the property. This helps ensure the property does not fall into disrepair or become subject to foreclosure, which could ultimately affect the future interest holders.

## Restrictions on Encumbrances

<!-- As a life tenant, you **cannot encumber the property beyond the duration of your life**. This means you cannot take actions that would affect the property's ownership or financial status after your passing, ensuring the property can seamlessly transfer to the next designated owner or interest holder. -->

A life tenant **cannot encumber the property beyond the duration of the life** estate. Actions cannot affect ownership or financial status after the tenant’s death, ensuring the property transfers seamlessly to the next designated owner or interest holder.
