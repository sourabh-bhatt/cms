# Personal Property Rights in Real Estate

<!-- When learning about **property rights**, it's important to understand **personal property rights**. These rights include different types of movable property. -->

When learning about **property rights**, understanding **personal property rights** is necessary. These rights include different types of movable property.

## Components of Personal Property Rights

<!-- ## Key Components of Personal Property Rights -->

- **Intellectual Property**: This property type can be protected by **patent** or **copyright**, protecting creations like inventions, books, art, designs, and symbols.

- **Business Reputation (Goodwill)**: An intangible asset that represents the value of a business's reputation and customer relationships.

- **Leases**: A lease gives someone the **right to use** property without owning it. The tenant gets possession rights, but the landlord keeps ownership.

- **Partnership Interests**: This means owning part of a business partnership with two or more people.

Understanding personal property rights helps real estate professionals work with different types of property. These rights affect how property is owned, used, and transferred.
