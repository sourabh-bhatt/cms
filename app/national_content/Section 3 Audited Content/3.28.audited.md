# Justifiable Reliance and Estoppel Defense in Property Rights

<!-- Property rights include the concept of **justifiable reliance**, especially in the context of an **estoppel defense**. -->

## What is Justifiable Reliance?

<!-- Justifiable reliance means that your decision to depend on a **promise** or **representation** about a property was reasonable and sensible. This means your reliance was based on facts, not guesses. Your reliance was based on what someone told you or showed you. -->

Justifiable reliance means a decision to depend on a **promise** or **representation** about a property is reasonable and supported by facts. It occurs when reliance is based on what was stated or shown, rather than on assumptions.

## Importance of Justifiable Reliance in Estoppel Defense

<!-- - Imagine someone made a promise or led you to believe something about a property that influenced your decisions.
- If you relied on that promise to make important decisions, your reliance must be justifiable.
- This justifiable reliance is crucial for an **estoppel defense**, which can stop someone from breaking their promise if you have changed your position based on their promise. -->

- A promise or representation about a property may influence decisions.
- If decisions are made based on that promise, the reliance must be justifiable.
- Justifiable reliance is central to an **estoppel defense**, which can prevent a party from breaking a promise when another has changed position based on it.

## Why It Matters

- The estoppel defense promotes **fairness** by protecting those who have acted in **good faith**.
- It balances the relationship between what is said, what is believed, and the actions taken.
- This concept helps in property disputes.
