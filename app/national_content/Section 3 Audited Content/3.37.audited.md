# Express Reservation in Property Transactions

<!-- When you're dealing with property transactions, it's crucial to understand the concept of **express reservation**.  -->

## What is Express Reservation?

**Express reservation** is when a grantor keeps certain rights when transferring property to a grantee. This means the grantor can reserve specific rights, such as an easement, while transferring ownership of the land to the grantee.

## How Express Reservation Works

- The grantor explicitly states the reservation of rights in the deed.
- These rights can include access to walk across the property or use water sources on it.
- The reservation lets the grantor keep these rights after the property transfer.

## Example

If a grantor transfers a large plot of land but wants to keep a path through it to reach another property they own, they can use express reservation to retain that right. By explicitly stating this in the deed, they protect their interests and ensure continued access.
