# Economic Characteristics of Real Property: Assemblage and Plottage Value

## Assemblage

**Example:** Imagine you come across two or more parcels of land lying next to each other. By combining these parcels into a single larger one under a single owner, you can boost the overall value of the property. This strategy is especially powerful when the resulting combined property is more valuable together than the total value of the individual parcels when they were separate.

## Plottage Value

The additional value that arises from this process of assemblage is called **plottage value**. **This principle can maximize the potential and profitability of land**, essentially making the whole greater than the sum of its parts.
