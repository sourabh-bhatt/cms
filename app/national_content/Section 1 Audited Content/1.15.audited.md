# External Obsolescence and Economic Obsolescence in Real Estate

**External forces can impact property values** beyond the property's own condition or the quality of its surroundings. These could be environmental mishaps, significant social changes, or even broad economic shifts. This concept is known as **external obsolescence**.

**External obsolescence** is an element outside the control of any property owner and can significantly impact a property's worth.

## Economic Obsolescence: A Specific Type of External Obsolescence

Economic obsolescence occurs when the decline in property value is directly tied to local adverse economic situations. Examples include:

- A failing local job market, which leads to decreased demand for housing in the area.
- New developments that attract potential buyers away from the neighborhood where your property is located.
