# Unearned Increment in Real Estate

This term refers to a scenario where the value of a property increases because of factors beyond the control of the property owner.

## What is Unearned Increment?

- **Unearned increment** occurs when external factors cause a property's value to rise.
- These factors are outside the owner's influence or investment.

## Examples of Unearned Increment

- The local government decides to **rezone** the area surrounding a property.
- Construction of a new **expressway** nearby.

Such actions can significantly boost the property's value without the owner having to lift a finger or invest more money into the property.
