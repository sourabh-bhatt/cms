# The Principle of Conformity in Real Estate

## Understanding Conformity

**In a typical residential area, homes are designed for family living**, with multiple bedrooms, backyards, and located near schools. If a property were introduced into this area that deviates significantly from this setup, such as a large commercial warehouse, it wouldn't just stick out; it would likely be less valuable in that specific context. This is because the warehouse does not *conform* to the established use and character of the neighborhood.

## Why Conformity Matters

**Conformity helps evaluate properties** not just on their individual merits but also in the context of their environments. When properties conform to the expectations and standards of their surroundings, they tend to be more desirable and, consequently, more valuable.

Key points to remember:

- **Conformity** influences property desirability and value.
- Properties that align with neighborhood use maintain or increase value.
- Non-conforming properties may decrease in value due to mismatch with surroundings.
