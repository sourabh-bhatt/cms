# Understanding Liquidity and Real Estate

## What is Liquidity?

When learning about real estate, you need to understand the concept of **liquid assets**, or in simpler terms, **liquidity**. Think of liquidity as a gauge that measures how quickly and effortlessly you can turn an asset into cash.

- Think of assets that can quickly turn into cash - those are your **liquid assets**.
- A shining example is **cash in a savings account** - it's already cash and immediately available.

## Liquidity in Real Estate

**Real estate sits at the opposite end of the liquidity spectrum** from highly liquid assets.

- Traditionally, **real estate** is seen as a **long-term investment**.
- It is not something that can be easily and quickly converted into cash compared to assets like cash in a savings account.
