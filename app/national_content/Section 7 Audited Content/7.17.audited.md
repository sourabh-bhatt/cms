# Graduated Payment Mortgage (GPM)

A **Graduated Payment Mortgage**, often abbreviated as **GPM**, is a type of fixed-rate loan designed to ease the initial financial burden on homeowners.

## How a Graduated Payment Mortgage Works

- During the first few years, typically the initial five, the payments are structured to be **negatively amortized**. 
  - This means the initial payments are intentionally kept low, lower than the interest accruing on the loan.
  - This feature is attractive for buyers who expect their incomes to increase over time.

- After this negatively amortized period, the payment structure shifts:
  - Payments are recalibrated to include both **interest** and **principal**.
  - This causes the payments to increase.
  - This transition marks the shift to **amortization**, where payments gradually pay off the loan over its remaining term.

## GPM Benefits

A Graduated Payment Mortgage offers a path for homeowners to ease into their financial responsibilities during the early years of their mortgage.
