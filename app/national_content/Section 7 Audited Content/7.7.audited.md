# Reverse Annuity Mortgage (RAM)

A **Reverse Annuity Mortgage**, or **RAM**, is distinctive because unlike traditional mortgages where the borrower makes payments to the lender, in a RAM, the lender makes payments to the homeowner.

## How a RAM Works

- The lender makes payments to the homeowner, which can be:
  - Periodic payments
  - Provided as a line of credit
- These payments are based on the **equity** the homeowner has accumulated in the property.
- These payments may have tax implications that borrowers should discuss with a tax professional.

## Key Features

- The **balance owed** on a RAM will never exceed the value of the home.
- The balance becomes due under specific circumstances, such as:
  - The death of the borrower
  - When the home is sold

## Who Uses a RAM?

- RAMs are often utilized by **elderly or retired homeowners**.
- It serves as a means to supplement or provide income by leveraging the equity in their homes without needing to sell them.

A RAM offers a financial solution for homeowners looking to access their home's equity without selling the property.
