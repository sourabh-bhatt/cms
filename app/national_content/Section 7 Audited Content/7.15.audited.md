# Package Mortgages in Homebuying

A **package mortgage** is one option in homebuying financing.

## What is a Package Mortgage?

A package mortgage is a type of loan where the borrower doesn't just use the house as **collateral**, but also includes **personal property**, often appliances or other items that will be part of the new construction home.

## Benefits of Package Mortgages

This type of mortgage offers a comprehensive way for borrowers to finance not just the house itself but also the items that make the house livable and comfortable from the start. 

- It is appealing for those looking to move into newly constructed homes.
- It eliminates the need to secure separate financing for big-ticket items that borrowers will immediately need.

**Package mortgages** provide an alternative financing option for homebuyers who need to include personal property in their loan.
