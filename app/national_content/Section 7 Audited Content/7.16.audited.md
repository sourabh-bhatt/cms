# Package Mortgage: Combined Collateral in Home Loans

A **package mortgage** combines real estate and personal property as collateral in home loans.

## What is a Package Mortgage?

In a package mortgage, not only is the **house itself used as collateral**, but additional **personal property items** within the home are included too. 

Example: If a buyer is purchasing a house and also getting a washer, dryer, and refrigerator with it, these items can be bundled into the mortgage. The loan encompasses both the **real estate** and the **personal property**, making everything part of the collateral for the loan.

## Benefits of a Package Mortgage

- Includes both real estate and personal property as collateral
- Opens up more negotiation options for buyers and sellers
- Provides additional financing flexibility in the real estate market

Package mortgages expand the possibilities for structuring home loans and negotiating terms.
