# Fully Amortized Loan

A **fully amortized loan** involves making consistent, equal payments at regular intervals, often monthly. The goal is to reduce the **loan balance** and all accrued **interest** to zero by the time the loan reaches its **maturity date**.

## Payment Structure and Composition

- Each payment amount remains steady throughout the life of the loan.
- The composition of each payment changes over time:
  - Initially, a larger portion of each payment goes toward **interest**, reflecting the larger initial **principal**.
  - As payments continue, the interest component decreases.
  - The proportion applied to the principal increases because the principal balance is reduced.

This shift occurs because as the **principal** decreases, there is less balance to incur **interest** on, allowing more of each payment to go directly toward reducing the **loan balance**.

This payment structure affects how the loan balance reduces over time.
