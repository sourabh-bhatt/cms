# Interest Only Mortgages

## Definition and Overview

An **interest only mortgage**, sometimes referred to as a **straight mortgage** or **term loan**, is a type of loan where the borrower pays only the interest during the loan's term. 

## Key Features

- **Payments:** During the loan term, payments cover only the interest.
- **Principal:** No payments are made toward reducing the principal amount borrowed.
- **Monthly Payments:** This results in lower monthly payments at the start of the loan.
- **End of Term:** The entire principal amount becomes due at the end of the loan term.

## Considerations

The borrower must be prepared to pay off the full principal once the term concludes.
