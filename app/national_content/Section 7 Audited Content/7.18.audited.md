# Partially Amortized Loans and Balloon Payments

A **partially amortized loan** involves making regular, equal payments over a period, which cover both the **interest** and a portion of the **principal**.

## Key Features of a Partially Amortized Loan

- Throughout most of the loan term, borrowers make consistent monthly payments that are lower than fully amortized loans.
- These payments cover interest and part of the principal.
- At the end of the loan term, a **balloon payment** is due.

## The Balloon Payment

- The **balloon payment** is a single, large payment that covers the entire remaining balance of the loan.
- This payment is larger than the previous monthly payments.
- It occurs at the loan's maturity, marking the end of the loan term.

## Financial Implications

- Choosing a partially amortized loan means reduced monthly payments during most of the loan's lifespan.
- This can be attractive for borrowers who want lower initial payments.
- However, the large balloon payment requires careful budgeting and financial planning to cover this final installment.

Example:

A borrower with a 5-year partially amortized loan with monthly payments of $1,000 covers interest and some principal with these payments. At the end of 5 years, the borrower might owe a balloon payment of $50,000, which is the remaining principal balance that was not paid down during the monthly payments.
