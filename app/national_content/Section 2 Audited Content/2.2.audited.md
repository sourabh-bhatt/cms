# Assemblage and Plottage Value in Real Estate

Example: Consider a city block that includes four equally sized lots. One of these lots has been on the market for over a year, listed at **$200,000**. A developer comes along who isn't interested in just one lot; they're eyeing the entire block. They approach the listing agent with an offer: **$1,000,000** for all four lots.

## Assemblage

The strategy of combining these lots into a single ownership is called **assemblage**.

## Plottage Value

As these individual lots merge into one, their combined worth is more than the sum of their parts. This boost in value from uniting the lots is **plottage value**.
