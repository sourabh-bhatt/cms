# Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) in Real Estate

This process helps provide an informal estimate of a property's **market value** without being a formal appraiser. As a **real estate professional**, performing CMAs becomes a key part of the professional role, offering valuable insights to clients. 

## What is a CMA?

A CMA involves comparing the **subject property** with similar properties that:

- Have recently sold
- Are currently on the market
- Were on the market but didn't sell within a reasonable period

This comparison helps in understanding what the market might be willing to pay for a certain property.

## Importance of Performing a CMA

- Performing a CMA is the one instance where a **broker** or **salesperson** is permitted to estimate the value of real estate for a client.
- This capability is an essential tool in the professional skillset, enabling effective guidance for clients through their real estate transactions with informed **pricing strategies**.
