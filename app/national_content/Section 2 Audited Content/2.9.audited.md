# Understanding Potential Gross Income in Rental Properties

This is essentially the maximum rental income a property can generate when all its units are fully occupied. It represents an ideal scenario where there are no vacancies. Alternative terms include **projected gross income** and **scheduled gross income**.

## What is Potential Gross Income?

When calculating this figure, the sum total represents all possible rent payments receivable in a year if every apartment, house, or unit in the property were rented out at full market rate, without any interruptions. This estimate does *not* account for any real-world issues such as:

- Vacancies
- Tenants being unable to pay rent

## Importance of Potential Gross Income

- This figure sets the benchmark for understanding the financial potential of a property.
- Calculating and interpreting **Potential Gross Income** provides a foundation for evaluating the viability and profitability of a rental property.
