# Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) Explained

## What is a CMA?
A **Comparative Market Analysis (CMA)** is an informal comparison that real estate agents use to recommend the best listing or offering price for properties. A CMA compares properties that have recently sold.

## The Essence of CMAs
CMAs help you compare properties in the market. They help you price a property by looking at similar properties that sold nearby. You compare properties that are similar in:

- Location
- Size
- Age
- Style
- Amenities offered

You may need to adjust your comparisons for differences between properties.

## Equations You'll Use
Here is a common calculation you will use. To find the best listing price using three comparable sales:

1. Add the total sales prices of the comparables.
2. Divide the total by the number of comparables to get the average selling price.

**Example:**
- Comp 1 sold for $275,000
- Comp 2 sold for $325,000
- Comp 3 sold for $295,000

Adding these amounts gives a total of $895,000. Dividing this total by 3 (the number of comparables) results in an average selling price of approximately **$298,333.33**.
