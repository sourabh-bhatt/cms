# Understanding Competition in the Real Estate Market

## How Competition Affects Profits

- When a particular **market segment** starts showing increased or excessive **profits**, it naturally draws the attention of **competitors**.
- This influx of similar competitors can lead to a decrease in profits for everyone involved, as the market becomes more **saturated**.

## The Positive Side of Increased Competition

- Sometimes, the entry of more competitors into a market can actually attract more customers.
- **Example:** In a **shopping center** scenario, as more stores open within it, the variety and options attract a larger crowd of shoppers.
- This increased **customer base** can offset the potential decrease in profits due to heightened competition.
