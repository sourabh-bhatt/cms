# Certificate of Title in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When you're exploring the process of acquiring or transferring property, one key concept you'll need to understand is the **Certificate of Title**. The **Certificate of Title** represents the final opinion of a **title examiner**, who, after a thorough search, determines whether the property's title is **marketable** or free from **defects**. This certificate functions in real estate transactions as a determination of title status.

## Function of the Certificate of Title

<!-- - When a buyer seeks to finance their property purchase through a loan, **mortgage companies** usually require **title insurance** to protect against potential title issues. -->
- When buyers seek to finance property purchases through loans, **mortgage companies** usually require **title insurance** to protect against potential title issues.
- The **Certificate of Title** underpins the issuance of this insurance, serving as a foundational document that asserts the status of the property's title.
<!-- - It assures all parties involvedâ€”especially the lender and the buyerâ€”that the title to the property is clear. -->
- It assures all parties involved - especially the lender and the buyer - that the title to the property is clear.
- This assurance means the purchase can proceed without legal entanglements related to ownership disputes or claims.
