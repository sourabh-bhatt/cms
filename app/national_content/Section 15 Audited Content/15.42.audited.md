# Understanding Real Estate Compensation

<!-- When you're entering the world of real estate, it's essential to understand how **compensation** works. -->
Real estate professionals need to understand how **compensation** works.

## Compensation Flow

<!-- - As a **salesperson** or **associate broker**, you'll receive your compensation directly from the **principal broker**.

- **Salespersons** or **associate brokers** receive compensation directly from the **principal broker**.
- This structure exists because the **principal broker** secures the payment from the **client**.
- The payment flow is as follows: the **client pays the broker**, and then the **broker pays the agent**.

## Negotiating Your Compensation

<!-- Negotiating your compensation is a key step that will take place between you and the **principal broker**. This discussion determines how much you'll be paid for the transactions or services you provide. -->
Negotiating compensation takes place between agents and the **principal broker**. This discussion determines payment for transactions or services provided.

<!-- - Your ability to negotiate effectively can significantly impact your **earnings** in this field. -->
- The ability to negotiate effectively can impact **earnings** in this field.

Example: If you negotiate a higher commission split with your principal broker, your income from each transaction will increase accordingly.
