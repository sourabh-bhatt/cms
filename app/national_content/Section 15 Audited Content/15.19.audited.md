# Fiduciary Duties and Responsibilities of a Real Estate Agent

<!-- When you become an **agent**, you will need to understand the significant **responsibility** you hold toward your **clients**, whether they are **sellers** or **buyers**. As an agent, you will owe **fiduciary duties** to them. Real estate **agents** hold **responsibility** toward their **clients**, whether **sellers** or **buyers**. Agents owe **fiduciary duties** to clients. This means agent actions should always be in client **best interest**, prioritizing client needs, **confidentiality**, and **financial interests** over others', including their own.

<!-- By understanding and embracing these responsibilities, you can ensure that your clients receive the highest standard of **service** and advice, fostering **trust** and successful relationships. -->
Understanding and embracing these responsibilities ensures clients receive the highest standard of **service** and advice, fostering **trust** and successful relationships.
