# Roles of Agents in Virginia Real Estate Transactions

<!-- In Virginia, when you step into the world of real estate transactions, you'll have the option to serve as an **agent** in three distinctive representation roles, depending on your preference and the needs of your clients. These roles are: -->
In Virginia real estate transactions, agents can serve in three distinctive representation roles, depending on preferences and client needs. These roles are:

- **Seller's Agent**
- **Buyer's Agent**
- **Dual Agent**

<!-- Each of these positions carries its unique set of responsibilities and functions within **residential transactions**. Each of these positions carries its unique set of responsibilities and functions within **residential transactions**. These different representation roles affect how agents represent clients and serve their best interests throughout the buying or selling process.
