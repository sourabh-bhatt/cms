# Understanding the Universal Agent in Real Estate

Understanding the **types of agency** is a concept in real estate. One type is the **Universal Agent**.

## What is a Universal Agent?

A universal agent has the power to perform many different tasks for another person. This level of authority is broader than that of other agents.

- A **Special Agent** is tasked with a specific duty.
- A **General Agent** handles ongoing business activities for a client in a specific area.
- A **Universal Agent** has broad authority to act for a client in all matters, often through a power of attorney that allows the agent to act in nearly any capacity on behalf of the client.

## Authority and Legal Implications

This expansive power can be a double-edged sword. The courts tend to approach **universal agency** with caution due to the extensive authority bestowed upon the agent.
In this role, the agent possesses the capability to legally bind clients in a wide array of commitments.

## Responsibilities and Boundaries

The responsibilities and boundaries of a **Universal Agent** help agents navigate their authority and serve clients' best interests.
