# Disclosure of Material Facts in Property Transactions

<!-- As a **real estate licensee**, it is crucial to disclose **material facts** that you are aware of, or reasonably should be aware of, to your clients and customers. This disclosure requirement often works hand in hand with, or sometimes independently of, any state-mandated **Property Disclosure Forms**. **Real estate licensees** must disclose **material facts** they are aware of, or reasonably should be aware of, to clients and customers. This disclosure requirement often works hand in hand with, or sometimes independently of, any state-mandated **Property Disclosure Forms**. Virginia Code § 54.1-2131 requires licensees to disclose material facts.

## What Are Material Facts?

Material facts are any information that could influence a buyer's or seller's decision about a property. Examples include:

- **Structural issues**
- **Pest infestations**
- **Legal disputes** over the property

<!-- These facts are important because they can impact the value of the property. They can also affect the willingness of a party to proceed with the transaction. -->
These facts can impact the value of the property and affect the willingness of a party to proceed with the transaction.

## Disclosure Requirements

Failing to disclose material facts is not just poor service. It can have serious legal consequences:

<!-- - Violation of the **Virginia Real Estate License Law**. This can put your license at risk.
- Exposure to **common law fraud** claims. This can lead to legal liabilities including damages. -->
- Violation of the **Virginia Real Estate License Law**, which can put licenses at risk
- Exposure to **common law fraud** claims, which can lead to legal liabilities including damages
- Violation of **Virginia Real Estate Board regulations**

## Ethical and Professional Responsibilities

The duty to disclose material facts goes beyond avoiding legal trouble. It involves:

- Maintaining **integrity** and **professionalism**

- Protecting your **reputation** and career -->
- Building **trust** with clients
