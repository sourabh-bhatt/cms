# Understanding Customers and Clients in Real Estate Transactions

When an agent represents a **seller** as a **client**, anyone interested in buying is considered the **customer**. As the seller's **agent**, the primary responsibility lies in serving the seller's interests.

Similarly, when representing a **buyer** as a **client**, the **seller** becomes the **customer**.

In property management, the **property owner** is typically the **client**, and **renters** are **customers**.

**Points to Understand:**

- **Clients** receive fiduciary duties and full representation
- **Customers** receive fair and honest treatment but not fiduciary duties
- Identifying the **client** and **customers** in each transaction is necessary
- Agent roles and responsibilities depend on whether they represent the seller, buyer, or act as a property manager
- Managing and prioritizing interests correctly ensures ethical real estate practice

These distinctions guide how agents manage responsibilities and prioritize interests.
