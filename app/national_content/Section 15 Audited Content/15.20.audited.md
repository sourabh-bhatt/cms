# Ministerial Acts When Representing Buyer Clients

<!-- When representing **buyer clients**, you might find yourself in situations where you can assist **sellers** or potential seller clients through what are known as **ministerial acts**. -->
When representing **buyer clients**, agents may assist **sellers** or potential seller clients through **ministerial acts**.

## What Are Ministerial Acts?

<!-- Ministerial acts are tasks or services you can perform for sellers that are purely **administrative** or **clerical** in nature. -->
Ministerial acts are tasks or services agents can perform for sellers that are purely **administrative** or **clerical** in nature.

## Considerations

- Performing ministerial acts, unless explicitly prohibited by law or the terms of the **brokerage agreement**, does **not** automatically establish a **brokerage relationship** with these sellers or potential sellers.
- These acts allow agents to assist sellers without forming a professional relationship or representing them beyond those specific tasks.

**Example:**
If a buyer's agent provides a seller with a simple information form or directions to a property, these are ministerial acts that do not create a brokerage relationship with the seller.
