# Understanding Income Properties and Key Financial Metrics

<!-- Let's dive into understanding **income properties**, a critical concept if you're looking to maximize your investment returns. **Income properties** represent a key concept for maximizing investment returns. Key metrics for income properties include **net operating income (NOI)** and **capitalization rates**. These metrics guide evaluation of property performance and value.

## Key Financial Calculations

<!-- Here's a fundamental breakdown of the equations you'll rely on: -->
Fundamental equations for income property analysis:

- **Annual Effective Gross Income**: Calculate by subtracting any vacancies and credit losses from the Annual Gross Income.
- **Annual NOI**: Subtract Annual Operating Expenses from the Annual Effective Gross Income.
- **Property Value**: Divide the Annual NOI by the Annual Rate of Return.
  - Conversely, multiplying the Property Value by the Annual Rate of Return gives the Annual NOI.
- **Annual Rate of Return**: Calculate by dividing the Annual NOI by the property's Value.

## Example: Calculating Rent with a Percentage Lease

Example: Evaluating a **percentage lease** where the rent is set at $1,000 per month plus 5% of gross sales. If the tenant's annual gross sales are $350,000, calculate the total annual rent using the formula:

> (Rent Per Month × 12 Months) + (Gross Sales × 0.05) = Annual Rent

Steps:

1. Calculate the base annual rent:
   - $1,000 × 12 = $12,000
2. Calculate the landlord's share of the annual gross sales:
   - $350,000 × 0.05 = $17,500
3. Add these together to find the total annual rent:
   - $12,000 + $17,500 = $29,500

## Example: Estimating Property Value Using Income Approach

Example: Assessing the value of an office building based on its income. The building generates $156,000 in gross income with annual expenses of $43,000, using a 7% capitalization rate.

Steps:

1. Calculate the annual NOI:
   - $156,000 - $43,000 = $113,000
2. Convert the capitalization rate to a decimal:
   - 7% = 0.07
3. Divide the annual NOI by the capitalization rate to find the property value:
   - $113,000 / 0.07 = $1,614,285.71
