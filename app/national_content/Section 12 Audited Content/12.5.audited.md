<!-- To understand how to maximize your investments in the real estate market, you need to grasp the concept of **Effective Gross Income (EGI)**. -->
Maximizing investments in the real estate market requires understanding **Effective Gross Income (EGI)**.

## What is Effective Gross Income?

<!-- Imagine you own a rental property. The total amount you charge in rent represents your **potential income**. However, it's rare to collect every penny of that due to various factors such as: -->
Example: Consider owning a rental property. The total amount you charge in rent represents your **potential income**. However, it's rare to collect every penny of that due to various factors such as:

- Vacancies
- Tenants who can't pay

<!-- The **Effective Gross Income** is what you actually end up receiving. It's your real income after you subtract any losses from vacant units or unpaid rent. -->
The **Effective Gross Income** is what property owners actually receive. It represents real income after subtracting losses from vacant units or unpaid rent.
