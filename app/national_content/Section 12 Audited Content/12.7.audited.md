# Investment Permanence (Fixity) in Real Estate

<!-- When you're exploring the world of investments, it's essential to grasp the concept of **Investment Permanence**, also known as **Fixity**. This economic principle sheds light on the enduring nature of investment in **infrastructure improvements** such as: -->
**Investment Permanence**, also known as **Fixity**, describes the enduring nature of investment in **infrastructure improvements** such as:

- Sewage systems
- Drainage systems
- Electricity systems

Because these types of improvements are intertwined with the **land** they enhance—which itself cannot be moved—they tend to generate stable, long-term returns. 

Unlike a singular improvement to a piece of property, like adding a residential home or a commercial building, investing in infrastructure opens up a broader spectrum of future development possibilities.

<!-- Understanding this principle can significantly impact your approach to **investment strategies**, allowing you to see the bigger picture and the lasting value that infrastructure improvements can bring. -->
This principle impacts **investment strategies** by revealing the broader perspective and lasting value that infrastructure improvements provide.
