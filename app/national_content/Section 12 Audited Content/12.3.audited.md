# Disintermediation in Investment

<!-- When you invest your own money directly into opportunities that promise higher yields, such as **money markets** or **government securities**, instead of depositing it into a bank, you are practicing what's known as **disintermediation**. This term might sound complex, but it simply means bypassing the middleman—in this case, the bank. -->
Investing money directly into opportunities that promise higher yields, such as **money markets** or **government securities**, instead of depositing it into a bank, is known as **disintermediation**. This term means bypassing the middleman - in this case, the bank.
