# Judgment Liens in Real Estate

A **judgment lien** is a **general** and **involuntary lien**. This means it can attach to a debtor's **real property** once a court has issued a judgment and the judgment is properly recorded.

## Scope of a Judgment Lien

- In Virginia, a judgment lien covers **real property** located within the county or city where the judgment is recorded.
- If a creditor wants to expand the lien to other counties or cities, they must record the judgment in those additional jurisdictions.
- Recording the judgment ensures the creditor's right to claim is recognized across different locations.

## Enforcement of Judgment Liens

The enforcement process involves a specific legal procedure:

1. If the debtor fails to satisfy the debt, the court can issue a **writ of execution**.
2. This writ authorizes the **sheriff** to seize and sell the debtor's property, both real and personal.
3. The sale proceeds are used to pay off the debt and cover the expenses associated with the sale.

**Judgment liens** and their enforcement processes apply when dealing with properties that might be subject to such claims. This knowledge helps navigate real estate transactions.
