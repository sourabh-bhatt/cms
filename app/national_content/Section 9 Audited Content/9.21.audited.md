# Understanding Lien Priority in Real Estate

<!-- When you're diving into the world of **liens**, it's crucial to understand how the **priority of a lien** can impact the order in which **creditors** are paid if the property is sold through **foreclosure**. Think of lien priority as a queue or lineup where liens are organized to determine who gets paid first, second, and so on. -->
The **priority of a lien** impacts the order in which **creditors** are paid if the property is sold through **foreclosure**. Lien priority functions as a queue where liens are organized to determine who gets paid first, second, and so on.

## The Hierarchy of Liens

The order of these liens follows specific rules:

- **Tax liens:** Tax liens usually have first priority by law. This means tax liens are paid before any other types of liens.
- **Recording date:** Most other liens get priority based on when they are **recorded**. The first lien recorded gets paid first.

## Lien Priority Applications

<!-- Understanding lien priority is essential because it determines the likelihood of a lien being paid off if the property is sold. The higher the priority, the greater the chance of the debt being recovered. This knowledge can shape decisions surrounding: -->
Lien priority determines the likelihood of a lien being paid off if the property is sold. The higher the priority, the greater the chance of the debt being recovered. This knowledge can shape decisions surrounding:

- Property investments
- Negotiations
- Resolving financial disputes related to property ownership
