# Understanding Property Tax Liens

<!-- When diving into the world of real estate, understanding **liens**, especially **property tax liens**, is crucial. Let's break down what a property tax lien really means for you. -->
**Property tax liens** represent a specific type of **lien** that affects real estate transactions.

## What Is a Property Tax Lien?

- A **property tax lien** is *parcel-specific*, meaning it is tied directly to a particular piece of real estate. It attaches itself to one distinct parcel, making its influence on that property direct.

- These liens are **statutory**, which means they arise from laws and legislation established by the government to ensure the collection of taxes.

- Property tax liens are **involuntary**. This means no one requests their placement; they occur automatically by law when property taxes are unpaid. The property owner does not have to sign anything, nor does the government need special steps beyond the normal tax assessment process.

## When Do Property Tax Liens Arise?

- Property tax liens are typically filed against real property at the start of each tax year, with Virginia using **January 1** as the effective date.

- The **effective date** of the lien is when the taxes are assessed, *not* when they become delinquent or when the lien is recorded.

## The Priority of Property Tax Liens

- Property tax liens hold **superiority** over all other liens, regardless of when those other liens were recorded.

- This hierarchy ensures that back taxes on a property are paid first before any other debts tied to the property.

## How Property Tax Liens Become Liens

- Overdue property taxes automatically become liens without the need for a separate filing or recording process.

- This transition reinforces the government's right to secure its dues.

## Property Tax Lien Characteristics

- Property tax liens are **parcel-specific**.
- They arise from **legal statutes**.
- They occur **involuntarily**.
- They take effect with the **tax assessment date**.
- They have **priority** over all other liens.
