# Tax Liens and Lien Priority in Real Estate

## Understanding Tax Liens

A **Tax Lien** is a **general**, **statutory**, and **involuntary lien**. It arises when there is a non-payment of taxes, such as:

- Federal estate taxes
- Federal income taxes
- Payroll taxes

If these taxes are not paid, the government can place a lien on the debtor's assets. This type of lien is comprehensive, meaning it can attach to *all real and personal property* owned by the debtor.

## Priority Among Different Types of Liens

**Priority** among different types of liens operates as follows:

- **Real estate tax liens** and **special assessment liens** generally take priority over **federal tax liens**, except for federal tax liens that were recorded before the real estate tax lien.
- This means that in the order of claim settlements, real estate taxes are usually paid before any claims from federal tax liens.

### Exceptions to the Rule

- **Federal estate tax liens** have priority over most other liens, but real estate tax liens for taxes assessed before the person's death still take priority.
- This priority affects the order in which debts are settled from the property's sale proceeds.

Lien priority determines which debts are paid first when a property is sold in real estate transactions.
