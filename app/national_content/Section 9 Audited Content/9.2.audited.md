# Understanding Liens and Their Priority

When dealing with **liens**, **creditors** must record the necessary document immediately after the lien is created. This step establishes the **priority** of the lien.

## Priority of Liens

- Liens typically follow a **"first in time, first in right"** approach regarding their treatment and enforcement.
- This means the order in which liens are recorded determines their priority.
- However, certain liens have statutory priority regardless of recording date (such as real estate tax liens).

## Lien Priority in Practice

This concept directly impacts the order of payment in the event of a **debtor's asset liquidation**. The lien recorded first generally gets paid before those recorded later.

Example: If two creditors have liens on the same property, the creditor who recorded their lien first will have priority in payment over the other.
