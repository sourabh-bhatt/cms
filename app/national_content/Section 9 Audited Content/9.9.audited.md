# Understanding Liens and Satisfaction in Real Estate

## What is a Lien?
A **lien** acts as a kind of *encumbrance*, or obstacle, on a property's title. This means that the property cannot be considered free and clear until the lien is removed.

## Satisfaction of a Lien
The process of **satisfaction** involves the removal of a lien once the underlying debt has been fully paid off. The property owner must ensure that the creditor who placed the lien completes and records a **satisfaction of lien** promptly after the debt is paid. This action:

- Clears the *cloud on the title*.
- Makes the property title clean again.

## Proof of Satisfaction: The Satisfaction of Lien Document
The proof that a lien has been satisfied and removed comes in the form of a **satisfaction of lien** document. This document:

- States that the lienholder no longer has any claim on the property.
- Confirms the clearance of any encumbrances attached to the property's title due to the lien.
- Is recorded in the public records to provide notice that the lien has been released.

This process ensures the property title is free and clear - a consideration in maintaining or transferring ownership without complications.
