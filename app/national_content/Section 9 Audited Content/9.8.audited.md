# Deficiency Judgment in Foreclosure and Trustee Sales

When a property goes into **foreclosure** or is sold via **trustee sale** and the proceeds from the sale are not enough to cover all the claims against it, a **deficiency judgment** may result.

## What is a Deficiency Judgment?

A deficiency judgment is not just a claim against the property itself, but a **personal judgment** against the individual(s) who failed to keep up with their **mortgage** or **deed of trust (DOT)** payments. This judgment can also apply to other parties responsible, such as:

- The original **borrower** who allowed another party to assume the mortgage or DOT
- **Endorsers**
- **Guarantors**

## Financial Responsibility Beyond the Property

If the property sale does not cover the amount owed, these individuals may still be held accountable for the remaining debt.

This concept demonstrates the financial repercussions that can extend beyond the property itself to the individuals involved.
