# Estoppel Certificates in Real Estate Transactions

In real estate transactions, parties may encounter an **estoppel certificate**. This document - also referred to as a **certificate of no defense** or an **estoppel letter** - clarifies the amount of **debt** one party owes to another or the **legal status** between parties as of a specific date.

## Use by Lenders

- Lenders rely on estoppel certificates to confirm the **outstanding mortgage amount** as of a certain date.

## Use by Landlords and Tenants

- Landlords and tenants use these certificates to verify the exact amount of **rent due** (or confirmed as not due) as of a defined date.

## Legal Effect

- Once an estoppel certificate is acknowledged as valid by all involved parties, it effectively prevents any of those parties from asserting a contrary claim afterward.

This mechanism ensures **transparency** and **agreement** on financial and legal statuses in real estate transactions.
