# Understanding Liens in Real Estate

In real estate, the concept of **liens** affects property transactions. Liens can impact property sales and purchases in various ways.

## Types of Liens

### Mechanic's Liens
- These liens are **specific** and arise from **statute**.
- They are applied **involuntarily**.
- Typically become effective once work begins on a property.
- If someone has worked on a property and hasn't been paid, they may file a mechanic's lien as a claim for payment.
- In Virginia, mechanic's liens must be filed within specific time limits and require proper notice.

### Judgment Liens
- These are **general liens** that arise from **statute**.
- Also applied **involuntarily**.
- Occur when a court awards a **monetary judgment** against a property owner who does not pay.
- The winning party can register a judgment lien against the property.
- When the property is sold, the lien must be settled before a **clear title** can be transferred.

### Attachment Liens
- Involve the property being seized and held pending the outcome of a **lawsuit**.
- Also called **lis pendens** liens in Virginia real estate practice.
- Ensure that if the lawsuit ends in a monetary judgment, assets will be available to satisfy the judgment.

These liens affect real estate transactions and require consideration when managing or advising on property sales and purchases.
