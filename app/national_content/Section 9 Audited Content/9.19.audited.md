# Attachment Liens in Real Estate

<!-- When dealing with **liens**, it's important to understand **attachment liens**. -->
**Attachment liens** are a legal measure where a court orders the seizure of someone's real and personal property. This action is taken to hold the property as collateral while a lawsuit is pending.

## Attachment Liens on Real Property

For **real property**, such as land or buildings, this attachment happens when a **writ of attachment** is recorded in the **public records**. This act creates a **lien** against the property which serves a specific role:

- It alerts everyone about the claim against the property.
- It ensures that the property can be used to satisfy any judgment that may be rendered in the lawsuit.

## Attachment Liens vs. Secured Obligations

<!-- It's crucial to understand that the process of attachment does **not** apply to **secured obligations**, like a **mortgage**. -->
The process of attachment does **not** apply to **secured obligations**, like a **mortgage**. Instead, the following occurs:

1. The lender (the party that lent the money for the property) must first try to recover the debt by selling the mortgaged property through a process known as **foreclosure**.
2. If, after the sale, there is still a portion of the debt unpaid, the lender then has the option to pursue an attachment of the debtor's other properties.

## Attachment Liens in Real Estate Transactions

These concepts apply when legal claims against a property might affect its sale, purchase, or transfer. **Attachment liens** can influence the handling and outcome of real estate transactions.
