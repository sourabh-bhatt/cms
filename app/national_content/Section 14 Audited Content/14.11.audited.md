# Estimating Monthly Mortgage Payments Using Loan Repayment Factors

<!-- When working with buyers in real estate, it's essential to estimate their **monthly mortgage costs** accurately to determine what kind of payments they can afford. Working with buyers in real estate requires accurate estimation of **monthly mortgage costs** to determine affordable payment levels. This process involves using a **loan repayment factor**, also known as an **amortization factor**, which helps calculate the monthly **principal and interest** payment with reasonable precision.

## What is a Loan Repayment Factor?

- A loan repayment factor is typically expressed as a dollar amount per $1,000 of the loan.
- For example, it might be stated as "$5.00 per $1,000."
- This factor simplifies the calculation of monthly payments by standardizing the amount owed per thousand dollars borrowed.

## Calculating Monthly Principal and Interest (PI)

To find the monthly **principal and interest (PI)** payment, use the following formula:


Monthly PI = (Loan Amount ÷ $1,000) × Loan Repayment Factor


### Example Calculation

Suppose a buyer is taking out a $250,000 loan, and the loan repayment factor is $7.50 per $1,000. Here's how to calculate the monthly PI:

1. Divide the loan amount by $1,000:
   
   $250,000 ÷ $1,000 = 250

2. Multiply the result by the loan repayment factor:

   250 × $7.50 = $1,875.00

Therefore, the monthly principal and interest payment would be **$1,875.00**.
