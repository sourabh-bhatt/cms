# Understanding Percentages in Real Estate Financing

<!-- Understanding **percentages** and their conversion plays a crucial role in **real estate financing**. When working with figures such as **commission rates** or **minimum down payments**, knowing how to handle percentages effectively is key. **Percentages** and their conversion apply to **real estate financing**. Working with figures such as **commission rates** or **minimum down payments** requires effective percentage handling.

## What is a Percentage?

<!-- A **percentage** represents a part out of 100. Whenever you come across any task involving percentages, you might need to convert numbers from decimal form to percentage or the other way around. This skill is vital in calculating various financial aspects in real estate transactions. -->
A **percentage** represents a part out of 100. Tasks involving percentages may require converting numbers from decimal form to percentage or vice versa. This skill applies to calculating various financial aspects in real estate transactions.

## Converting Percentages and Decimals

<!-- To convert a percentage to a decimal, you have two easy methods: -->
Converting a percentage to a decimal involves two methods:

- Move the decimal point two places to the left
- Divide by 100

For example, **1%** becomes **1/100** or **0.01** when converted into a decimal.

Conversely, turning a decimal into a percentage involves:

- Moving the decimal point two places to the right
- Multiplying by 100

Thus, **0.01** becomes **1%** after conversion.

## Using Percentages in Calculations

When dealing with word problems involving percentages, the term "**of**" usually signals multiplication. For example:

- To find **16% of 1,000**, multiply **0.16 × 1,000 = 160**.

<!-- Sometimes, you need to reverse the operation and divide to find the percentage a number represents of another. For instance: -->
Reversing the operation involves dividing to find the percentage a number represents of another. For instance:

- To find what percent **160** is of **1,000**, divide **160 ÷ 1,000 = 0.16**, which is **16%**.

## Real Estate Financing Examples

**Example 1:**

If a buyer is purchasing a house for **$80,000** and decides to make a **25% down payment**, how much would that payment be?

1. Convert **25%** to a decimal by dividing by 100: **0.25**.
2. Multiply the house price by this decimal: **$80,000 × 0.25 = $20,000**.

Thus, the down payment would be **$20,000**.

**Example 2:**

If a buyer makes a **$20,000 down payment** on an **$80,000** house, what percentage of the house price does this down payment represent?

1. Divide the down payment by the house cost: **$20,000 ÷ $80,000 = 0.25**.
2. Convert this decimal back into a percentage by multiplying by 100 or moving the decimal two places to the right: **25%**.

Therefore, the down payment is **25%** of the house price.
