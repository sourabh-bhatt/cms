# Pre-Qualification in Real Estate Financing

**Pre-qualification** is an informal estimate of how much money a buyer might be able to borrow for a home loan.

## Pre-Qualification Process

- Lenders usually do pre-qualification over the phone or online.
- Pre-qualification does not require documents or paperwork.

## Pre-Qualification Benefits

Pre-qualification helps buyers:

- Know how much they can borrow before they start looking at homes.
- Focus on homes they can afford.

Example: A buyer calls a lender and gives basic financial information. The lender gives an informal estimate of the loan amount the buyer might qualify for. This helps the buyer look at homes within their budget.
