<!-- When you work with **mortgages** in real estate financing, you will work with calculations and story problems about financing real property. You must understand how these calculations work. They are important for understanding mortgage financing. -->
**Mortgages** in real estate financing involve calculations and story problems about financing real property. These calculations help with understanding mortgage financing.

## Components of Mortgage Calculations

These calculations usually involve:

- Determining **monthly payments**
- Calculating **interest rates**
- Understanding how **amortization schedules** work
- Estimating a **borrower's eligibility** based on their financial status

Each component helps show what a mortgage will look like for a potential homeowner.

## Calculating Monthly Payments

<!-- Understanding how to calculate monthly payments is your starting point. -->
Calculating monthly payments involves knowing:

- The **principal amount** borrowed
- The **interest rate** agreed upon
- The **term of the loan**

Monthly payment calculations help clients understand what they will pay each month and help clients make decisions.

Interest rates affect monthly payments and the total amount paid over the loan term.

## Amortization Schedule

An **amortization schedule** is a detailed breakdown of each payment throughout the life of the mortgage. It shows how much of each payment goes towards the principal versus interest. <!-- When you can read and explain amortization schedules, you can help clients understand how their equity builds over time. -->
Amortization schedules help clients understand how their equity builds over time.

## Borrower's Financial Eligibility

Gauging a borrower's financial eligibility involves looking at:

- Income
- Debts
- Credit score
- Other financial obligations

Lenders check financial health and decide loan amounts and interest rates based on these factors.
