# Conventional Loans in Real Estate Financing

<!-- When diving into the realm of real estate financing, you'll encounter various types of loans. One of the key categories you should understand is **conventional loans**. -->
Real estate financing includes various types of loans. **Conventional loans** represent one such category.

## What Are Conventional Loans?

- These are loans that **primary market lenders** originate without the backing of government insurance, such as that offered by the **Federal Housing Administration (FHA)**, or guarantees from the **Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)**.
<!-- - Instead of relying on government support, conventional loans are significantly influenced by the **underwriting policies** of **secondary market investors**, including: -->
- Instead of relying on government support, conventional loans are influenced by the **underwriting policies** of **secondary market investors**, including:
  - **Fannie Mae (FNMA)**
  - **Freddie Mac (FHLMC)**

## Risk and Terms of Conventional Loans

- Because conventional loans lack government assurance, they present a **higher risk to lenders** compared to government-backed loans.
- This increased risk can affect the **terms and conditions** under which these loans are offered.
