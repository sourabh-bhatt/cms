# Understanding Equity in Real Estate Financing

<!-- Equity is the **cash value** you have in a property after settling all your debts, including any mortgage you might owe. It represents your **financial stake** in the property and is a crucial concept in real estate financing. -->
Equity is the **cash value** in a property after settling all debts, including any mortgage owed. It represents the **financial stake** in the property.

## How to Calculate Equity

The formula to calculate equity is:

**Equity = Current Market Value - Outstanding Loan Balance**

### Step-by-Step Breakdown

- <!-- Suppose your property has a **current market value of $100,000**. -->
Suppose a property has a **current market value of $100,000**.
- <!-- You currently owe **$70,000** on your mortgage. -->
The owner owes **$70,000** on the mortgage.

<!-- To find your equity: -->
To find the equity:

1. Take the current market value of the property: $100,000

<!-- 2. Subtract what you still owe on the mortgage: $100,000 - $70,000 = $30,000 -->
2. Subtract what is owed on the mortgage: $100,000 - $70,000 = $30,000

<!-- This $30,000 represents your equity in the property. -->
This $30,000 represents the equity in the property.
