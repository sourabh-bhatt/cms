# Understanding Discount Points in Real Estate Financing

<!-- In the world of real estate financing, you'll encounter the concept of **discount points**, which are crucial for you to understand. -->
Real estate financing includes the concept of **discount points**. 

## What Are Discount Points?

<!-- **Discount points** are essentially additional fees that lenders charge borrowers. This fee allows borrowers to secure interest rates on their loans that are lower than the market rate. Think of discount points as a trade-off – you pay more upfront at closing to pay less in interest over the life of the loan. -->
**Discount points** are additional fees that lenders charge borrowers. This fee allows borrowers to secure interest rates on their loans that are lower than the market rate. Discount points represent a trade-off - borrowers pay more upfront at closing to pay less in interest over the life of the loan.

- Discount points are usually expressed as a percentage of the total loan amount.
<!-- - One discount point equals **one percent of your loan amount**. -->
- One discount point equals **one percent of the loan amount**.

<!-- For example, if you're dealing with a loan of $100,000, one point would cost you $1,000. -->
Example: With a loan of $100,000, one point would cost $1,000.

## How Discount Points Affect Interest Rates

<!-- You might wonder how many points it takes to reduce your interest rate. Typically, **one discount point** reduces the interest rate by approximately **0.25%**. -->
Typically, **one discount point** reduces the interest rate by approximately **0.25%**.

### Example:

- Suppose the lender needs to achieve a **10% yield** on a loan to make it worthwhile.
<!-- - As a borrower, you want a **9% interest rate** for the duration of your loan.
- To bridge this gap, the lender can charge **four discount points at closing**.

By doing so, the lender effectively secures the desired 10% yield, while you enjoy a lower interest rate of 9% over the life of your loan. -->
- The borrower wants a **9% interest rate** for the duration of the loan.
- To bridge this gap, the lender can charge **four discount points at closing**.

By doing so, the lender effectively secures the desired 10% yield, while the borrower enjoys a lower interest rate of 9% over the life of the loan.
