# Understanding Leverage in Real Estate Investment

<!-- When you use **borrowed funds** to finance an investment, this is known as **leverage**. Essentially, the more you borrow, the more **highly leveraged** you become. -->
Using **borrowed funds** to finance an investment is known as **leverage**. The more an investor borrows, the more **highly leveraged** they become.

The goal of leverage is to earn a **profit** that exceeds both the initial **purchase price** of the property and the **cost of borrowing**. This strategy can amplify earnings, provided the investment performs well.

**Points about leverage:**

- Leverage involves using borrowed money to increase the potential return on investment.
- The higher the leverage, the greater the risk and potential reward.
- Successful use of leverage depends on the investment generating returns that surpass borrowing costs.

Leverage can maximize **investment returns** in the **real estate market**.
