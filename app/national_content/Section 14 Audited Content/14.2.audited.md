# Intermediation in Real Estate Financing

<!-- Intermediation is a process you'll encounter frequently in the world of **real estate financing**. It involves financial institutions, known as **intermediaries**, utilizing funds that have been deposited by their customers. -->
Intermediation is a process frequently encountered in **real estate financing**. It involves financial institutions, known as **intermediaries**, utilizing funds deposited by their customers.

## How Intermediation Works

<!-- - When you deposit money into your checking or savings account, the funds do not simply sit idle. -->
- When customers deposit money into checking or savings accounts, the funds do not simply sit idle.
- The bank takes these deposits and invests them in various opportunities, such as:
  - **Money markets**
  - **Mortgages**
  - **Government securities**
  - Other investment vehicles
