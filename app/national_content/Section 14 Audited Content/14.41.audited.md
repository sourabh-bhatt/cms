# Savings and Loan Associations in Real Estate Financing

<!-- When diving into the realm of real estate financing, it's crucial to understand the role of **Savings and Loan Associations (S&Ls)**. -->
**Savings and Loan Associations (S&Ls)** are financial institutions in real estate financing. These financial institutions promote **home ownership** and encourage private savings.

## Difference from Commercial Banks

S&Ls differ from commercial banks by offering **higher interest rates on deposits**, which can be a reason for individuals to choose them for their savings and mortgage needs.

## Charter and Regulation

- S&Ls must receive a **charter** from either the **federal government** or the **state** where they're based.
- If federally chartered, they are regulated by the **Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC)**.
- Their accounts are insured by the **Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC)**, protecting depositor funds up to **$250,000 per individual account** within each institution.
- Most state-chartered associations participate in the federal insurance program.

## Lending Activities

<!-- A significant portion of an S&L's operations revolves around making loans, especially **conventional loans** directed at **residential properties with one to four units**. -->
S&L operations focus on making loans, especially **conventional loans** for **residential properties with one to four units**.

- **Conventional loans** are **not part of government programs**.
- S&Ls also facilitate loans with government backing, such as those under **FHA** and **VA Programs**, which can be beneficial depending on client circumstances.

## Secondary Mortgage Market

S&Ls originate mortgages and engage in **buying and selling mortgages on the secondary market**. This activity influences the availability and terms of mortgages, thereby impacting the broader real estate market.
