# Understanding Usury in Real Estate Financing

<!-- In real estate financing, it's essential to understand the concept of **usury**. -->
**Usury** is a concept in real estate financing. This refers to the practice of charging an **interest rate** on a loan that exceeds the **legal limit** set by **state usury laws**.

## Virginia Usury Laws

- Virginia sets a **maximum interest rate** of **12% per year** for most loans.
- **Mortgage loans** may be subject to different rates or exempt under federal regulations.
- These laws are designed to protect borrowers from high rates that could make a loan unaffordable.
- Virginia usury laws work alongside **federal regulations** that may override state limits for certain loan types.

## Usury Law Functions

<!-- Knowing about usury is crucial because it: -->
Usury laws:

- Protect **borrowers** from predatory lending practices.
- Ensure **lenders** adhere to fair and legal lending standards.
- Promote **fair lending practices** within the real estate market.
