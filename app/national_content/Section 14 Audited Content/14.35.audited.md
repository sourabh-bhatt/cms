# Financial Institutions in Real Estate Financing

<!-- As you explore the world of **real estate financing**, it's important to understand the role of **financial institutions**. Picture these institutions as intermediaries, or middlemen, who play a crucial part in the finance ecosystem. -->
**Financial institutions** serve as intermediaries in **real estate financing**. These institutions gather funds from **depositors** and lend these funds to **borrowers**. The institutions earn a return through **interest rates**.

## Common Types of Financial Institutions

- **Commercial Banks**: These are prevalent and offer a wide range of financial services.
- **Savings and Loans Institutions**: Traditionally focus on **residential mortgages**.
- **Insurance Companies**: Not always directly associated with banking but can be lenders in **commercial real estate**.
- **Credit Unions**: Member-owned and often provide personalized financial services.
