# Understanding Discount Points in Real Estate Financing

<!-- Let's learn about **discount points** in real estate financing. -->
**Discount points** are fees in real estate financing. A borrower may want to get a mortgage at an interest rate below current market rates. To do this, the borrower can pay extra fees to the lender when the loan closes. These fees are called **discount points**.

## What Are Discount Points?

<!-- Discount points are optional fees you can pay to lower your mortgage interest rate. -->
Discount points are optional fees borrowers can pay to lower their mortgage interest rate. Each discount point equals **1% of the loan amount**. <!-- When you buy points, you are **prepaying interest** to get a lower rate. -->
When borrowers buy points, they **prepay interest** to get a lower rate.

- Typically, purchasing **one point** reduces the interest rate by **0.25%**.

## Calculating the Cost of Discount Points

The formula to calculate the cost of discount points is:

**Loan Amount × Number of Points = Point Dollar Amount**

### Example Calculation

Example: Consider a $50,000 loan where the borrower wants to purchase **2.5 points**.

1. Convert points to decimal: 2.5 points = 2.5% = 0.025
2. Multiply loan amount by decimal points:

   \[ 50,000 \times 0.025 = 1,250 \]

<!-- This means you would pay **$1,250** in points to lower your interest rate. -->
This means the borrower would pay **$1,250** in points to lower the interest rate.

## Determining Number of Points from Amount Paid

<!-- If you know the amount paid in points and want to find the number of points purchased, use the formula: -->
If the amount paid in points is known, use this formula to find the number of points purchased:

**Point Dollar Amount ÷ Loan Amount = Number of Points**

### Example

Example: If a borrower pays **$1,250** in points on a $50,000 loan:

  \[ \frac{1,250}{50,000} = 0.025 \]

- This equals **2.5%** or **2.5 points**.
