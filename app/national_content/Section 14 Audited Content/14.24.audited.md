# The Role of Mortgage Bankers in Real Estate Financing

<!-- Mortgage Bankers are essential players in the real estate financing world. Think of them as **primary lenders**; these can be individuals, firms, or corporations that take a unique step in the lending process. -->
Mortgage Bankers are **primary lenders** in the real estate financing world. These can be individuals, firms, or corporations that take a unique step in the lending process.

## How Mortgage Bankers Operate

- They begin by borrowing funds from **commercial sources**.
- Instead of lending these funds directly in the conventional sense, they use them to finance both **conventional loans** and **government-backed loans**.
- Rather than holding onto these mortgages, mortgage bankers **sell them at a discount to investors on the secondary market**.

This process might seem like a magic trick, but it is a strategic part of their business model.

## Loan Closing and Risk

- When mortgage bankers close loans, they do so **in their own name**, meaning they assume all the risks associated with the loan until it is sold.
- This risk assumption influences how mortgage bankers operate and make decisions.

## Loan Servicing

- Even after selling loans on the secondary market, mortgage bankers often continue to **service the loans**.
- Loan servicing includes managing day-to-day responsibilities such as:
  - Collecting payments
  - Managing escrow accounts
  - Handling other administrative tasks related to the mortgage

## Distinction from Savings & Loans and Commercial Banks

- Unlike **Savings & Loans (S&Ls)** and **commercial banks**, mortgage bankers **do not take in deposits**.
