## Collateral Assessment

<!-- In addition to evaluating your financial stability, lenders assess the value of the property you want to purchase. -->
Lenders assess the value of the property to be purchased. This process, known as **collateral assessment**, ensures that:

- The property's worth adequately covers the loan amount.
- The lender has a safety net in case of default.
