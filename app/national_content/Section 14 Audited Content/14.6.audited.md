# Understanding Mortgage Brokers and Mortgage Bankers

## Role of Mortgage Brokers

<!-- When discussing **mortgage brokers**, it's important to understand their role as *intermediaries* in the real estate financing process. They act as the middlemen who facilitate the connection between **borrowers** and **lenders**. Unlike mortgage banks, brokers: -->
**Mortgage brokers** serve as *intermediaries* in the real estate financing process. They act as middlemen who facilitate the connection between **borrowers** and **lenders**. Unlike mortgage banks, brokers:

- Do **not** directly lend money
- Do **not** handle loan servicing

Instead, mortgage brokers focus on:

- Qualifying borrowers and properties
- Reaching out to lenders to arrange financing

<!-- As a real estate agent working with mortgage brokers, you should understand that brokers: -->
Real estate agents working with mortgage brokers should understand that brokers:

- Help assess the client's financial situation
- Help evaluate the real estate involved
- Help ensure both meet the lender's requirements

Once a borrower is matched with a suitable lender and a loan arrangement is made, brokers are compensated, usually through a fee paid by the borrower. This fee is often calculated as a **percentage of the loan amount**.

## Role of Mortgage Bankers

Mortgage banking operates differently. **Mortgage bankers**:

- Originate loans
- Fund loans directly

<!-- They play a significant role in **commercial real estate financing** and are especially involved in the origination of **FHA** and **VA loans**, which cater to specific borrower needs. -->
They play a role in **commercial real estate financing** and are especially involved in the origination of **FHA** and **VA loans**, which cater to specific borrower needs.
