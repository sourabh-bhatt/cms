# Sale-Leaseback in Real Estate Financing

A **sale-leaseback** is a real estate financing technique.

## What is a Sale-Leaseback?

A sale-leaseback works this way: A homeowner sells their property to an investor or lender. After the sale, the homeowner leases the property back from the new owner. The homeowner can stay in the home for a set period of time.

## Why Use a Sale-Leaseback?

This arrangement helps when:

<!-- - You have sold your home, but your next home is not ready yet.
- You are waiting for the purchase process of your new home to finish.
- The construction of your new home is still underway. -->
- The homeowner has sold their home, but the next home is not ready yet.
- The homeowner is waiting for the purchase process of the new home to finish.
- The construction of the new home is still underway.

<!-- A sale-leaseback agreement lets you stay in your current home until you are ready to move to the new one. -->
A sale-leaseback agreement allows the homeowner to stay in the current home until ready to move to the new one.

## Benefits of a Sale-Leaseback

<!-- - **Financial flexibility:** You get cash from the sale while staying in the home.
- **Continuity of residence:** You avoid moving twice or finding temporary housing. -->
- **Financial flexibility:** The homeowner receives cash from the sale while staying in the home.
- **Continuity of residence:** The homeowner avoids moving twice or finding temporary housing.

This technique offers both financial and practical benefits in certain real estate transactions.
