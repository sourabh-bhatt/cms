# Understanding PITI: The Four Components of a Mortgage Payment

<!-- When you're looking into real estate financing, one key concept you'll need to grasp is **PITI**. -->
**PITI** is a concept in real estate financing. This stands for **Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance**, which are the four main components of a typical mortgage payment. <!-- Here's how each part plays its role in your monthly financial commitments: -->
Each part plays a role in monthly financial commitments:

## Components of PITI

- **Principal**: <!-- This is the portion of your monthly payment that goes towards reducing the balance of your loan amount. Every payment you make lowers the principal and, in turn, reduces your debt. -->
This is the portion of the monthly payment that goes towards reducing the balance of the loan amount. Every payment lowers the principal and reduces the debt.

- **Interest**: <!-- Alongside the principal, you also pay interest, which is essentially the cost of borrowing the money. This is calculated based on the interest rate and the remaining balance of your loan. Think of it as the lender's fee for lending you the money. -->
Alongside the principal, borrowers pay interest, which is the cost of borrowing the money. This is calculated based on the interest rate and the remaining balance of the loan.

- **Taxes**: <!-- Property taxes are a necessary part of homeownership. A portion of your monthly payment will be set aside to cover annual property taxes owed to your local government. These funds are typically held in a reserve account, and when your taxes are due, the payment is made on your behalf. -->
Property taxes are a part of homeownership. A portion of the monthly payment is set aside to cover annual property taxes owed to the local government. These funds are typically held in a reserve account, and when taxes are due, the payment is made on the borrower's behalf.

- **Insurance**: <!-- Just like taxes, you'll also contribute monthly towards homeowners insurance, and if required, private mortgage insurance (**PMI**). Homeowners insurance protects your property against losses and damage, while PMI is needed if your down payment is less than 20% of the home's value, protecting the lender against the risk of loan default. -->
Like taxes, borrowers contribute monthly towards homeowners insurance, and if required, private mortgage insurance (**PMI**). Homeowners insurance protects the property against losses and damage, while PMI is needed if the down payment is less than 20% of the home's value, protecting the lender against the risk of loan default.

## How PITI Works

<!-- Each month, you'll make one payment that covers all these components. This collective payment is not only pivotal in managing your mortgage but also ensures that taxes and insurance are taken care of without any additional effort on your part. -->
Each month, borrowers make one payment that covers all these components. This collective payment manages the mortgage and ensures that taxes and insurance are taken care of.

<!-- Understanding **PITI** is fundamental because it gives you a clearer picture of the total monthly costs of owning a home, beyond just the loan amount, helping you plan your finances more effectively. -->
**PITI** provides a picture of the total monthly costs of owning a home, beyond just the loan amount, helping with financial planning.
