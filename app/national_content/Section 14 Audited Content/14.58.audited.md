# Understanding Seller Financing in Real Estate

<!-- When you're exploring the realm of real estate, understanding **seller financing** is crucial. Imagine a seller who decides to finance the sale of their property by lending you, the buyer, money directly. -->
**Seller financing** is a concept in real estate. A seller finances the sale of their property by lending the buyer money directly.

## What is Seller Financing?

**Seller financing** occurs when the seller acts as the lender, providing the buyer with the funds needed to purchase the property instead of the buyer obtaining a loan from a traditional financial institution.

## Features of Seller Financing

- The seller lends money directly to the buyer.
- The buyer makes payments to the seller according to agreed terms.
- This arrangement bypasses traditional lenders like banks.

## Benefits of Seller Financing

- **Flexibility:** Offers adaptable terms for both buyer and seller.
- **Opportunities:** Enables transactions that might not qualify for traditional financing.
- **Negotiation:** Allows for customized agreements tailored to the parties' needs.
