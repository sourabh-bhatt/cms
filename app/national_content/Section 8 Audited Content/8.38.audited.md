# Eviction in Lease Agreements

**Eviction** is the legal process of removing a tenant from rental property. Landlords must follow Virginia's legal process to evict tenants.

Eviction happens when a tenant breaks the lease agreement rules. Common reasons include:

- Not paying rent
- Violating lease terms
- Staying past the lease end date

In Virginia, landlords must follow specific steps including proper notice and court proceedings. The legal action may include:

- Suing for any harm caused
- Asking a court to terminate the lease

Eviction requires court approval through an **unlawful detainer action**. This process must be handled carefully and in accordance with proper **legal procedures**.

Eviction affects **lease agreement** management and ensures that both parties fulfill their obligations.
