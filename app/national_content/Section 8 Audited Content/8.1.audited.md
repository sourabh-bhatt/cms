# Lease-Purchase Agreements in Real Estate

When a tenant is interested in buying the property they are leasing but faces obstacles such as not qualifying for a loan or lacking sufficient funds for a down payment, a **lease-purchase agreement** can offer a solution. This type of agreement combines the elements of leasing with those of a purchase contract.

## How a Lease-Purchase Agreement Works

- Both the tenant and the landlord agree on a **set purchase price** for the property.
- They also agree on a specific **future date** when the sale will be finalized.
- During the period leading up to the purchase date, a portion of the tenant's **monthly rent payments** is allocated toward the purchase price.

This arrangement allows the tenant to work toward owning the property by making rent payments, part of which serves as an investment in their future home. It is advantageous for tenants who currently cannot qualify for a loan or do not have enough funds for a down payment but want to secure the opportunity to purchase the property in the future.
