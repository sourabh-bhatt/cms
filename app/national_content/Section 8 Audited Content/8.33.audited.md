# Revaluation Lease (Reappraisal Lease)

A **revaluation lease**, also known as a **reappraisal lease**, is a leasing arrangement where the rent can be periodically adjusted. This adjustment is based on the **revaluation** or **reappraisal** of the real estate in question.

## How Revaluation Leases Work

- The rent is adjusted to reflect changes in the property's value over time.
- Changes in value may result from **market forces** or **improvements** made to the property.
- Rent adjustments occur periodically during the lease term.

Revaluation leases affect how much rent tenants pay over time.
