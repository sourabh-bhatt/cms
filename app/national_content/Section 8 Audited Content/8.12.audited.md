# Impact of Voluntary Sales and Transfers on Leased Real Property

**Voluntary sales** and **transfers** of leased real property affect existing lease agreements in specific ways.

## Lease Continuity After Sale

- The **lease** is a **contractual agreement** that grants the tenant **personal property rights**.
- The sale of the property **does not automatically terminate the lease**.
- The **new owner steps into the shoes of the previous landlord** under the terms of the existing lease.

## Residential Leases and Cancellation Clauses

- Some residential leases include a **cancellation clause**.
- This clause allows the landlord to **end the lease early if the property is sold**.
- With a cancellation clause, the landlord can sell the property **free and clear of the lease**, enabling the new owner to start fresh without existing tenants.

## Sale Without a Cancellation Clause

- If there is **no cancellation clause**, the sale does **not** terminate the lease.
- The property is sold **subject to the encumbrance** (burden) of the lease.
- The lease continues, with the **new owner assuming the landlord's role**.
