# Long-Term Leases

**Long-term leases** are considered **interests in land**. 

## Written Leases

- If a lease is for more than one year, it must be **in writing** and **signed by both parties**.
- Without proper writing and signatures, the lease cannot be enforced in court.

## Purpose of Proper Documentation

Proper documentation serves to:

- Protect the rights of both the **tenant (lessee)** and **landlord (lessor)**.
- Ensure both parties understand their duties and responsibilities.
