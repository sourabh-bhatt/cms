# Graduated Leases and Index Leases in Real Estate

## Graduated Lease

A **graduated lease** involves rental increases at predetermined intervals. This type of lease provides scheduled rent adjustments, allowing landlords and tenants to anticipate changes in rental payments over time.

## Index Lease

A variant of the graduated lease is the **index lease**. In this lease type, rent is periodically adjusted either upwards or downwards based on the fluctuations of a specific index, such as the **Consumer Price Index (CPI)**. 

Key points about index leases include:

- Rent adjustments correspond directly to changes in the chosen index.
- There is often a **cap** on how much the rent can change during a given period, typically set at 4% or 6%.
- This cap ensures that rent changes remain within a manageable range for both landlords and tenants.

These lease types provide different approaches to managing rental payment changes over time.
