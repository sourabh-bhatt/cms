# Protective Covenants in Commercial and Retail Leases

A **protective covenant** is a lease provision used in commercial and retail spaces to address competition concerns.

## Protective Covenants

Example: A business owner looking to lease a space in a shopping center has concerns about **competition**. They worry about similar businesses moving into the same complex. A protective covenant addresses this concern.

## Role of the Protective Covenant

The **lessee** can request a protective covenant from the **lessor**, which is typically the landlord or the management company of the development. This covenant is a promise made by the lessor **not to lease any other spaces within the same development to direct competitors of the lessee**.

### Key Points about Protective Covenants:

- It serves as a **safeguard** for the lessee's business.
- Helps maintain a certain **exclusivity** within the development.
- Reduces **direct competition** nearby.
- Directly impacts the lessee's decision to commit to a lease.
- Can affect the **success** of their business in that location.

## Benefits

Protective covenants provide value in commercial and retail leasing arrangements by addressing competition concerns.
