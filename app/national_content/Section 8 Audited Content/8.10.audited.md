# Early Termination of Lease Agreements

A lease agreement can be ended early without penalty if both parties agree. If a tenant proposes to end the lease (also known as surrendering the lease) and the property owner (the lessor) agrees, the lease can be terminated, ending future rent payment obligations.

## Lease Agreement Provisions for Early Termination

Leases commonly include a **cancellation clause**. This clause is a pre-agreed condition that allows either the tenant or the lessor to terminate the lease under specific conditions, which might include paying a certain amount of money.

## Cancellation Clauses in Commercial and Industrial Properties

In commercial or industrial properties, cancellation clauses give both parties - the lessee or the lessor - the capability to cut the lease short as long as certain agreed-upon conditions are met or a predetermined sum is paid.

### Typical Payments and Costs Covered

The payment required upon cancellation is usually calculated to cover potential losses or expenses the non-canceling party might face due to the cancellation. These may include:

- Brokerage fees
- Costs associated with finding a new tenant
- Anticipated rental income lost because of the ended lease

## Lease Termination Benefits

Lease termination clauses provide benefits:

- Save both parties money
- Avoid potential legal disputes
- Ensure real estate dealings are conducted smoothly and professionally
- Keep both parties satisfied
