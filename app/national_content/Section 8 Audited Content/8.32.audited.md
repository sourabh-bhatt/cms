# The Escalator Clause in Leasing Agreements

An **Escalator Clause** in **leasing agreements** gives the **lessor** the ability to modify payments, either increasing or decreasing them, based on certain predefined conditions.

## Key Conditions Affecting the Escalator Clause

These conditions can include:

- Changes in **market conditions**
- **Cost of living adjustments**
- Fluctuations in **property taxes**
- Variations in **utilities** costs

The Escalator Clause can impact the financial commitments in a lease. This clause affects lease negotiations.
