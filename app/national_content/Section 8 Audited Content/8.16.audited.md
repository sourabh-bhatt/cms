# Short-Term Leases

**Short-term leases** are lease agreements with specific legal characteristics.

## What Are Short-Term Leases?

- Short-term leases are lease agreements set for **less than one year**.

## Key Characteristics of Short-Term Leases

- Unlike most leases that require a **formal written agreement** to be legally binding, short-term leases are an exception.
- These leases can be **oral** (verbal) and still be **enforceable**.

A **verbal agreement** with a tenant for a period of less than one year still holds **legal weight**.

This distinction affects how lease agreements operate legally.
