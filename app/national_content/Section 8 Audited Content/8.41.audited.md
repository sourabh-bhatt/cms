# Lease Contract Termination in Property Management

How **lease contracts** can be terminated affects **property management** and **real estate transactions**. Depending on the specific **leasehold** and the conditions outlined in the **lease agreement**, the pathways to ending a lease can vary considerably.

## The Power of the Lease Agreement

The **lease agreement** outlines the responsibilities and rights of both the landlord and the tenant and sets the stage for how and under what circumstances the lease can come to an end. Different lease agreements contain these clauses.

## Types of Leaseholds and Termination Scenarios

Each type of **leasehold** presents unique scenarios for termination:

- **Fixed-term lease**: Naturally expires after its predetermined duration.
- **Periodic lease** (such as month-to-month lease): Requires proper notice to terminate. In Virginia, month-to-month leases require 30 days written notice.

These lease types require proper handling.

## Methods of Lease Termination

Leases can end through various means:

- **Mutual agreement** between landlord and tenant.
- **Fulfillment of contract terms**, such as reaching the lease expiration.
- **Eviction** due to **breach of contract** or other legal grounds under the Virginia Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (VRLTA).

Each situation requires a blend of legal knowledge and interpersonal skills, ensuring professional handling of lease terminations regardless of the cause.

These concepts affect management of real estate transactions and property management aspects.
