# Estate at Will (Tenancy at Will)

An **Estate at Will** is a type of **leasehold agreement**. It is also called a **tenancy at will**. This happens when a tenant lawfully occupies property without a specific end date. There is no fixed schedule for rent payments, unlike month-to-month leases.

## Key Features of Estate at Will

- **Flexibility for both tenant and landlord:** This arrangement can be terminated by either party at any time with proper notice.
- **Virginia notice requirement:** In Virginia, either party must give at least 30 days written notice to terminate an estate at will.
- **Termination triggers:** Termination can occur through various events, such as:
  - The sale of the property
  - Death of either the landlord or tenant
  - One party giving proper notice to the other

## Estate at Will Characteristics

**Estate at Will** offers flexibility not found in other lease arrangements. This concept affects property dealings and provides an option that lacks the concrete boundaries of traditional leases.
