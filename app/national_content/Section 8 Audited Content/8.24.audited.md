# Covenant of Quiet Enjoyment in Leasing

The **_covenant of quiet enjoyment_** is a promise made by the **landlord** (also known as the **lessor**) to the **tenant** (or the **lessee**), assuring that tenants can enjoy possession of the property peacefully and without interference from others claiming to have a better or hostile right to the property.

## The Covenant

- The covenant does **not** guarantee that the property's title is flawless.
- Instead, it protects the tenant's right to **use and enjoy** the leased property without wrongful disturbance or eviction by someone with a superior ownership claim.

### Example
If you were evicted because someone else had a legally superior claim to the property, this would be a **breach of the covenant of quiet enjoyment**.

## Covenant in Leasing Agreements

This covenant often guarantees tenants the **exclusive right** to use and possess the property for a certain period. It also assures that tenants will be defended against hostile claims to possession.

## Landlord's Rights

While the covenant protects tenants' peaceful enjoyment, it does not remove all rights from the landlord. The landlord retains:

- The **leased fee title** - ownership interest in the property.
- The **reversionary interest** - the right to regain possession once the lease term ends.

This balance between tenant rights and landlord rights under the covenant of quiet enjoyment affects leasing experiences.
