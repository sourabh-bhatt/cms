# Tenancy for Years and Lease Expiration

Leases with a clear expiration date, known as **tenancy for years**, automatically come to an end on that specified date. This means:

- There is no need for either party - the landlord or the tenant - to give notice about ending the lease.
- The lease concludes as per the terms laid out from the start.

This automatic expiration affects lease transitions, including:

- **Renewals with current tenants**
- Marketing the property to **potential new tenants**
