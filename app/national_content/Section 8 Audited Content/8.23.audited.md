# Impact of Property Destruction or Condemnation on Lease Agreements

When a leased property faces **destruction** or **condemnation**, this impacts the **lease agreement**.

## Foundation of a Lease

The foundation of any lease rests upon tenant rights to:

- **Possess** the property
- **Use** the property
- **Enjoy** the property without disturbance

## Effect of Property Damage or Condemnation

If the property can no longer serve these purposes due to substantial damage or government seizure through eminent domain proceedings, the lease automatically terminates. This is because the property can no longer fulfill the basic conditions that justified the lease in the first place.

This concept affects lease management and situations where the property is compromised.
