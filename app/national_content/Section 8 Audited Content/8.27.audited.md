# Escalator Clause in Leases

An **escalator clause** in a lease dictates how the **rent** can increase under specific conditions.

## The Escalator Clause

- An escalator clause might state that if the property's **taxes** or **insurance premiums** rise by a predefined margin, the rent will also increase accordingly.
- This clause enables forecasting potential adjustments in lease costs.
- This clause affects lease management and client **financial commitments**.

Example: If a lease includes an escalator clause tied to property taxes, and the taxes increase by 5%, the rent may increase proportionally based on the terms outlined in the lease.
