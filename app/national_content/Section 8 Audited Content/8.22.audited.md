# Lessee's Interest in Leases

A **lessee's interest** is the exclusive right to **use and possess** a property. This right is granted through a **lease contract**. This right is considered **personal property**, often referred to as a **leasehold estate** or **non-freehold estate**.

## Key Concepts of Lessee's Interest

- The lessee acquires a **limited interest** in the real property.
- This interest grants **possession rights**, but **not ownership** of the property.
- The lessee can **live in or use** the property, but does **not own** the property itself.

This distinction lays the groundwork for understanding various types of leases and how **property rights** are transferred **without transferring ownership**. The difference between possession and ownership is a concept in real estate.

---

Example: If you rent an apartment, your lease gives you the right to live there (possession), but the landlord retains ownership of the property.
