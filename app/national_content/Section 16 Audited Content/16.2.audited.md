# Common Source Information Companies in Real Estate

<!-- When you work in real estate, understanding the role and function of **common source information companies** is crucial. These companies are called **multiple listing services (MLS)**. They collect and share information about real estate properties available for sale or lease, among other vital data. -->

**Common source information companies**, known as **multiple listing services (MLS)**, collect and share real estate property data for sale or lease.

## What is an MLS?

- MLS systems act as comprehensive databases of real estate listings.
- Real estate firms pay fees to access MLS systems.
- Each entry in the MLS provides detailed information about a property.

<!-- Think of MLS as large databases where you can find properties for your clients. -->

MLS functions as comprehensive databases containing property information for client searches.

## Benefits of Using MLS

<!-- By using the MLS, you can: -->

MLS provides several benefits:

- Streamlined property search processes.
- Access to detailed property information.
- Smoother real estate transactions.

- Providing comprehensive property options for clients.
- Supporting effective client service.
- Facilitating property matching and transaction completion.
